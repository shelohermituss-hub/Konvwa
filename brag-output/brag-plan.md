# Brag Plan: KONVWA

## What is this app?
KONVWA is a Haitian mobile-first import app that lets anyone order products from Alibaba, Shein, and Temu and pay in HTG via MonCash — no foreign card required.

## The angle
Fintech-grade polish for a market that's never seen it. The joke isn't stated — it lives in the gap between how premium KONVWA looks and what it's actually doing: replacing the visa-card-and-freight-forwarder nightmare with a phone tap.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen → the three logos slam in side by side: Alibaba · Shein · Temu. One beat of silence, then the KONVWA wordmark fades over them. The message lands before a word is spoken: we connect you to all three.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The submit form — user pastes an Alibaba product URL; the field auto-fills and a price in HTG appears below it. The number counts up in gourdes.
- The KONVWA PAY card on a dark navy background — the orange glow pulses once as the balance updates. Premium feel, unmistakably Haitian.
- A MonCash confirmation toast pops — green checkmark, "Paiement confirmé." No bank. No dollar. Done.

## Outro / punchline
The app icon drops center-screen, large. Under it: "L'import, enfin simple." Two-second hold, then fade.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: paste Alibaba/Shein/Temu URL into the submit form
→ Key action: price calculated automatically in HTG (gourde counter animates)
→ Result: MonCash payment confirmed, order tracked on dashboard

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: fintech app for Haiti that doesn't look like it's for Haiti
- Interpretation: Clean, confident, mobile-first pacing. Each scene holds just long enough to feel settled. No clutter, no hustle-culture energy — the restraint IS the statement.

## Format: landscape — 1920×1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: warm corporate bed with clean momentum; music carries the energy so visuals stay refined
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120.19 BPM, upbeat corporate pop)
- Music treatment: fade in at 0s over 0.5s, volume ~60%, gentle fade-out starts at 18s over 2s
- Music cue guidance: track has strong cues at 3.02s (hit the brand reveal), 16.02s (intensity peak 0.9988 — land the MonCash confirmation), 17.02s (intensity 1.0 — outro card drop). Beat grid at 120.19 BPM ≈ 0.499s per beat; use every-other-beat (≈1.0s) spacing for sequential text reveals.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS to make the KONVWA PAY card glow breathe slightly — no waveform bars
- SFX posture: sparse; motion-matched; professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments: price counter tick sounds as HTG number animates; soft UI confirmation chime on MonCash toast; subtle keyboard tick if URL typing is simulated
- Restraint rule: music must never overpower the product reveal; no bass drop, no swell above the card moment

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: The Three Giants — 3s
Three platform logos (Alibaba orange, Shein pink, Temu orange-red) slide in simultaneously from off-screen left/right/bottom. They settle centered. 0.8s hold. KONVWA wordmark fades in centered above them, white Poppins bold. Logos fade to muted.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three logos arrive simultaneously (same beat), then wordmark fades over the top
Audio intent: punchy arrival, then quiet confidence as the brand appears
Audio-coupled idea: logos snap on the downbeat at ~0.5s; wordmark fade on the cue hit at 3.02s
Music: warm upbeat corporate — building momentum
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal: KONVWA PAY Card — 4s
Dark navy background. The KONVWA PAY card rises from bottom-center with a subtle scale-in (0.95→1.0) over 0.4s. The card sits center-screen with its gradient and orange glow. Balance reads "24,500 HTG". Below the card, white sans text: "Votre wallet, en HTG." 2.5s hold.
Sequential/interaction: none — single card reveal
Audio intent: premium, settled — the product has arrived
Audio-coupled idea: card glow pulses once softly synchronized to the beat at ~4.02s
Music: full warm bed, stable
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Feature: Submit a Product URL — 5s
Phone-portrait mockup centered on dark background. The submit form is shown: URL field with a cursor blinking. Text types in: "alibaba.com/item/..." character by character over 1.2s with subtle key tick SFX. Below the field, a price row animates: "HTG ——" → "HTG 8,500" counting up over 0.8s. Label above: "Commander depuis Alibaba." 1.5s hold on settled state.
Sequential/interaction: yes — URL types out character by character, then price counts up
Audio intent: active, working, confident — the product is doing its thing
Audio-coupled idea: keyboard tick SFX on each character during typing; subtle counter tick sounds as HTG price counts up
Music: same warm bed
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Feature: MonCash Confirmation — 4s
Screen shows wallet page on dark navy. The MonCash button is visible. A simulated tap gesture (cursor/finger ripple) touches it. A green toast notification slides down from the top: checkmark icon + "Paiement confirmé" + the amount. 2s hold on the toast.
Sequential/interaction: yes — tap gesture → toast slides in
Audio intent: relief, completion — the hard part was easy
Audio-coupled idea: soft UI confirmation chime on toast arrival, synchronized to beat at ~16.02s (peak cue)
Music: same bed, approaching outro energy
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro / Punchline — 4s
Clean dark navy full-bleed. KONVWA logomark drops center at scale 1.2→1.0 over 0.3s. Under it, after 0.5s: "L'import, enfin simple." in white Poppins medium, letter-by-letter or fade-in. 2.5s hold. Music fades out. Fade to black.
Sequential/interaction: yes — logo lands first, tagline fades in 0.5s later on the 17.02s cue
Audio intent: satisfaction, minimalism — the product speaks for itself
Audio-coupled idea: tagline reveal on the 17.02s intensity cue (1.0 peak); music fades gracefully under the text hold
Music: fading warm bed
Transition mood: fade to black

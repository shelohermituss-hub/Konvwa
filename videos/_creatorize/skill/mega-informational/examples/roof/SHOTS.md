# "Build a roof" (new film on the Everest look): shot list. 66.75 s, 30 fps, frames 1-2003, 1080x1920.
Voice: film/roof/voice.wav (words: voice.json). Frame of a word = round(t * 30) + 1.

Same aesthetic as the Everest rebuild (engine/mega.py, the owner's Blender look): realistic EEVEE, deep blue sky
(storms where the words need weather), hard sun, crisp materials, GREEN X-ray on the part being named
(boards, shingles, underlayment, gutter, truss, ice dam, insulation, flashing), every shot a continuous flight with
whip blur into the next. ONE hero house all film long: a small stone-and-timber cottage on a green hillside
(the roof gets BUILT across the film: flat boards -> tilted -> shingles -> underlayment -> gutter -> truss ->
insulation -> flashing), so the viewer watches it grow. Weather is the antagonist: rain, wind, snow, ice.
Scale: 1 unit = 1 m. House 8 x 6 m, walls 2.8 m.

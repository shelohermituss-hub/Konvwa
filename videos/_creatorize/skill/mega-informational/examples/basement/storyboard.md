# How a Hole Becomes a Basement - storyboard

Library: searched (cutaway / soil / section / pitfalls). Nothing reusable for Blender Python; applied the know-how
pitfall.any.strata-stripes-read-as-planks (soil = lumpy clods first, faint wavy strata only).

## The idea, step by step
A hole under a house -> it sinks and caves in -> concrete footing -> concrete walls -> wet dirt pushes (15 cars) -> crack
-> steel bars inside -> water is the real enemy -> drain tile (perforated pipe + gravel) -> nowhere to go -> sump pit,
pump, float switch, discharge to the yard -> storm kills the power -> battery -> concrete is a sponge -> tar outside ->
the ground breathes radon -> cracks in the floor -> radon pipe under the slab + fan out above the roof -> the white pipe
on houses -> window, stairs, washing machine -> a basement.

## The set (one all film long)
One small front-gable house (8 x 7 m, white lap siding, grey shingles) on a green yard. 1 unit = 1 m. The world is CUT at
the plane y = -3.5 (the house's front facade): in front of it is air, so the camera (at -y) looks at the soil's cut face
and straight into the basement under the house. Soil block x +-16, y -3.5..30, z -14..0: brown soil in lumpy layers
(dark topsoil, brown loam, ochre clay, grey-brown gravel deep), grass on top, trees + fence behind. Low warm sun from the
front-left (sun_rot ~205-215, elev 22-30) rakes across the cut face. Water is blue. Highlights: green X-ray on the word.
Details all on the right (+x) side: drain tile, sump pit (front-right corner, cut in half by the section), battery,
radon pipe (up the outside of the right wall, fan, above the roof edge).

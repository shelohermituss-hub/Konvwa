# Everest rebuild: shot list (ref/frames, 1780 frames, 29.97 fps -> we render 30 fps, frame N = ref frame N)

The owner's own Blender short "Could You Move Mount Everest To America?". 1:1 rebuild. Every shot is a continuous
FLIGHT (no static holds): fast whip moves with heavy motion blur into and out of each shot (the cut hides in the blur).
Look (measured on the sheets): realistic EEVEE, NOT toon - no outlines. Deep blue clear sky with a few cumulus, a low
hard sun, bright snow (blue-white shadows), the slab = dark grey basalt cylinder with pitted texture + snow cap,
X-ray highlights = semi-transparent emissive GREEN (#3cff3c) for "the thing being talked about", copper/orange steel
machinery, red cracks of lava glow in the dark ground, ocean deep teal with sun glints, white bold captions (post.py).

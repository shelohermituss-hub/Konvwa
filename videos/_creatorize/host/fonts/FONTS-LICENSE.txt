The fonts in this folder hold only the accented letters and punctuation the styles' own fonts lack (made by
scripts/ext-fonts.py from Google Fonts). They are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org), except Special Elite (Apache License 2.0): Playfair Display, EB Garamond, Orbitron,
Share Tech Mono, League Gothic, Oswald, Archivo Black, Space Mono, Inter, UnifrakturMaguntia, Special Elite,
Pinyon Script, Courier Prime, Anton, Abril Fatface.

The *-viet.woff2 files hold the Vietnamese letters (ơ, ư and the letters with stacked tone marks) of the same fonts, made by
scripts/ext-fonts.py --viet from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1): Inter, JetBrains Mono, EB Garamond, Oswald,
Space Mono, Anton, Playfair Display, League Gothic, Pinyon Script.

The *-cyrl.woff2 files hold the Cyrillic letters (Russian and the rest of U+0400-045F, Ґ, Ү, Ұ, №), made by
scripts/ext-fonts.py --cyrl from Google Fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. From the same fonts: Inter,
JetBrains Mono, Open Sans, Oswald, Playfair Display, EB Garamond. A face whose font has no Cyrillic is given the letters of
an unmodified look-alike (the file's name is the face's, fonts.json "from" names the font inside): Orbitron - Tektur,
Share Tech Mono - JetBrains Mono, Space Mono - Martian Mono, League Gothic - Alumni Sans, Anton - Oswald, Abril Fatface -
Yeseva One, Archivo Black - Montserrat, Courier Prime - IBM Plex Mono, Special Elite - Podkova, Pinyon Script - Great
Vibes, UnifrakturMaguntia - Old Standard TT, Kalam - Pangolin.

script/ holds Noto Sans JP, Noto Sans KR, Noto Sans SC, Noto Sans Thai and Noto Sans Devanagari (Google, SIL Open Font
License 1.1, each with its OFL.txt), in the pieces Google Fonts splits them into, cut down to the Japanese, Korean,
Chinese, Thai and Devanagari letters by scripts/script-fonts.py from their Fontsource packages (@fontsource-variable).

orbitron-700-punct.woff2 is a Modified Version of Orbitron (SIL Open Font License 1.1, Reserved Font Name "Orbitron"),
named "Creatorize Marks": the quote marks « » ‹ › „ ‚, the middle dot · and the ordinals º ª drawn from Orbitron's own shapes by
scripts/orbitron-punct.py. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 too.

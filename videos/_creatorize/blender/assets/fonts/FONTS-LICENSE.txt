The fonts in this folder draw the 3D text and captions of the Blender engine in the letters Arial Black lacks. Each is
Inter Black (Latin, Latin Extended, Vietnamese, Cyrillic; from @fontsource/inter) merged with one Noto Sans script font at
weight 800 (Noto Sans Thai, Devanagari, JP, KR, SC; the pieces in the app's templates/host/fonts/script), built by the app's
scripts/blender-fonts.py and renamed "CZ Latin", "CZ Thai", "CZ Devanagari", "CZ Japanese", "CZ Korean", "CZ Chinese".

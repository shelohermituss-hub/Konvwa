# Personal Brand: Videos page and New video

platforms: both

The **Videos** page (Personal Brand sidebar) lists every video you planned: title, thumbnail and edit.

## New video
Click **New video** (top right, or at the bottom of the sidebar):
- **Title** (needed), e.g. "I let Claude edit my videos for a week".
- **Channel**: the channel it belongs to. Lessons kept for that channel on the YouTube page show here as a reminder.
- **Thumbnail idea**: what the thumbnail shows.
- **Core concept** (optional): the one idea the video delivers, the hook.
- **Thumbnail image** (optional): **Choose image…** (PNG, JPG or WebP), or make one later in Thumbnails.

Click **Plan video**. The editor opens: drop your footage in next.

You can change the core concept, thumbnail idea and thumbnail later in the editor's **Plan** tab.

## On the Videos page
- Search with **Search videos**. The channel picker shows one channel's videos; **Show every channel** or **Look in every channel** shows them all again.
- Click a video to open it in the editor.
- **Show folder** opens its folder.
- The channel chip on a video moves it to another channel.

## Delete a video
Click the bin (**Delete**). You can choose:
- **Remove, keep folder**: it leaves the app, its folder stays.
- **Move project to Recycle Bin** (Windows) / **Move project to Trash** (Mac): its project folder goes there.

Your footage is never deleted either way: the recordings stay where they are.

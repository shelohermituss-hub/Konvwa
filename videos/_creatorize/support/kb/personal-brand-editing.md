# Personal Brand: timeline and cuts
keywords: timeline, personal timeline, cut, cuts, split, split clip, trim, ripple delete, delete clip, s key, q key, w key, j k l, shortcuts, undo cut, redo, zoom timeline, fit timeline, scrub, playhead, frame by frame, next cut, mute, crossfade, pop at cut, click at cut, manual edit, fix the cut myself
platforms: both

The timeline under the preview shows the edit: **Video**, **Facecam** and **Audio**. Claude's cut lands here, and you can fine-tune it yourself.

## Basic moves
- Click or drag on the timeline to move the playhead.
- Click a clip to select it.
- Drag a clip's edge to trim it; the rest moves up to close the gap.
- The buttons above the timeline: undo, redo, split, zoom out, zoom in, fit.

## Keys
- Space: play / pause. J / K / L: back 2 s / pause / play (L again: 2×).
- S: split the clip at the playhead.
- Q: delete from the clip start to the playhead. W: delete from the playhead to the clip end.
- Delete (Mac) / Delete or Backspace (Windows): delete the selected clip.
- ← / →: one frame. Shift + ← / →: one second. ↑ / ↓: previous / next cut.
- Home / End (Windows) or fn ← / fn → (Mac): start / end.
- + / -: zoom the timeline. Alt/⌥ + scroll: zoom around the mouse. 0: fit. Middle-drag: move the timeline.
- Ctrl/⌘ + Z: undo. Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Z: redo.
- M: mute. ?: the shortcut sheet.

Deleting with Q, W or Delete closes the gap (a ripple delete).

## Good to know
- At every cut the sound crossfades over 20 ms so there is no click; the picture cuts straight.
- The line under the preview says which clip plays ("Clip 3 of 12") and where it comes from in the recording.
- Changes save by themselves (**Saved** at the top).
- Bigger changes: ask Claude in the **Claude** tab with **Change** (see "Personal Brand: Claude workflows").
- Undo a whole Claude cut with **Revert the cut** in the Claude tab.

# Ultraedit: upgrade shots
keywords: ultraedit, ultra edit, ultraedit+, ultraedit plus, upgrade shots, improve shots, better shots, more detail, polish, make it look better, shots look bad, ugly shots, fix visuals, premium, locked, premium feature, stop ultraedit, cancel ultraedit, undo ultraedit, how long, purple shots, pulsing shots, ultraedited
platforms: both

Ultraedit (Premium) goes through the shots you pick, one by one: Claude (Opus 5.5, or the model and effort picked for the project) looks at stills of each shot, adds detail and depth, fixes visual inconsistencies and checks the result. Timing and narration stay.

## Start one

1. Select shots in the **Shots** list or on the timeline (Shift + click for more). With nothing selected it starts on the shot you are on.
2. Click the **Ultraedit** button under the Pipeline. With shots selected it reads **Ultraedit shot 3** or **Ultraedit 4 shots**.
3. **Change shots…** shows every shot: **All**, **None**, **Not done yet**. Shots done before say **Ultraedited**.
4. Optional: **Notes for Claude** (type @ for a reference).
5. Click the **Ultraedit** button with the shot count.

It takes about 1-3 minutes per shot and uses more of your Claude plan. The shots being worked on pulse purple in the list and on the timeline; follow it in the Claude panel.

## Stop or undo

- **Stop** in the Claude panel ends it.
- The whole Ultraedit is one edit: **Revert** on its card undoes it exactly. Its card shows Before / After for each shot (arrows step through them).

## Ultraedit+ (3D reconstruction only)

Tick **Ultraedit+** in the dialog. Your picture service paints an ideal frame of each shot (free on Google Flow, otherwise about 1-5 cents a shot), then Claude rebuilds the shot to match it pass by pass, with a score out of 10. Pick the **Model** (Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5), **Effort** (Low, Medium, High) and **Time per shot** (No limit, or 1 to 10 min). About 5-15 minutes per shot.

## In a build

In the Build dialog, the top levels of the **Quality** slider add Ultraedit (and Ultraedit+ in 3D reconstruction) right after the build.

## Locked?

On Standard the button shows **Premium**: "Ultraedit is a Premium feature. Upgrade to Premium on Skool to unlock it." See "Plans: Standard and Premium".

Ultraedit always runs on Claude, even when the project is set to a ChatGPT model.

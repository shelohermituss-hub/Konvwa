# Video language: English or several languages

platforms: both

**Settings > General > Language** sets the language your videos are narrated in. Claude writes the script and the words on screen in it, the voices read it, and the app times its words.

## Options

- **English only** (default): new videos are in English. A video made in another language keeps it.
- **Several languages**: 32 languages, like Spanish, French, German, Polish, Russian and 27 more. Then:
  - **New videos start in**: the language new videos get. Each video has its own: set it in New project or in the editor.
  - A line says what transcribes that language on this computer.

## Transcription for other languages

When you switch to Several languages, Claude may start setting up transcription for them right away (it says so). Otherwise:

- "Nothing on this computer transcribes … yet: Whisper is used (slower).": click **Set up with Claude** (Claude must be signed in).
- "The setup for several languages did not finish": click **Try again**.
- "… doesn't do …: Whisper is used for it (slower).": click **Redo with Claude** to look for a model that also does it.

The progress also shows in **Settings > System > System check**.

## Good to know

- Other languages are a Premium feature. On Standard you see "Other languages is a Premium feature. Upgrade to Premium on Skool to unlock it."
- Each style writes only the languages its fonts have letters for; New project says which. Russian works in every style: a font without Cyrillic letters gets them from a close look-alike. 3D cartoon shorts on the Blender engine and 3D Informational write every language except Hindi.
- The free local voice doesn't speak Russian: use ElevenLabs or Fish Audio for a Russian voiceover.
- Arabic and other right-to-left languages are not offered yet.
- Personal Brand videos are edited in English, whatever you pick here.
- With several languages, **Settings > Media > Voiceover** keeps default voices per language (**Default voices for**).

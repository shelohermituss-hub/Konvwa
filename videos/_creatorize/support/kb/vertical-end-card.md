# Vertical end card (Shorts)
keywords: end card, end screen, outro card, logo, channel picture, avatar, profile picture, headline, watch the full video, call to action, cta, name on end card, channel name, remove logo, change logo, shorts ending, last seconds, video picture
platforms: both

The **End card** in **Export > Vertical 9:16** adds 3.5 s at the end of a vertical video, trailer or clip: where to watch the full video, your name and a picture. Switch it on and click the **End card** tab above the phone to see it.

## Headline

- Starts with the usual line for a trailer, a clip or a full video.
- Type your own (up to 60 characters): it carries over to your next videos.
- **Use the usual headline** brings the usual one back. Leave it empty for no headline.

## Name

- Starts with the channel's name when the video belongs to a channel, else a name you typed before, else the video's title.
- A channel name you type is remembered for your next videos (a title isn't).
- **Use the title** puts the title back. Leave it empty for no name.

## Picture

- **Logo…**: **Choose a logo** (PNG, JPG or WebP). It shows in a circle, like a channel avatar. One logo for all your videos: **Change** replaces it, **Remove** asks "Remove it from every video's end card?".
- **Channel picture**: shown instead when the video belongs to a channel. Change it on the **Channels** page.
- **Video picture**: the video's card picture with a play button.

Don't want one? Switch **End card** off. The length line under **Length** shows the total with the end card added.

# Logging in with Skool (membership)
keywords: skool, log in, login, log in with skool, sign in, signin, can't log in, cant login, won't open, app won't start, locked, not a member, membership, member, join, rejoin, another account, switch account, wrong account, checking your skool membership, stuck checking, no internet, can't reach skool, antivirus, firewall, vpn, plan, standard, premium, upgrade
platforms: both

Creatorize opens only for members of the **Creatorize: AI Video Mastery** community on Skool. When the app starts, a small window checks your Skool membership first.

## First start

1. The window shows **Checking your Skool membership...**
2. If you are not logged in, it shows **Log in with Skool**. Click the **Log in with Skool** button. Skool's own login page opens (Google login works too). Your password goes only to Skool.
3. After you log in, the window shows **Welcome** with your plan (**Standard** or **Premium**) and the app opens by itself.

You stay logged in. If you were checked within the last day, later starts open straight away without this window (the check runs in the background).

## The window says "Not a member"

The Skool account you logged in with is not in the community, so the app stays locked.

- **Join on Skool** opens the community page so you can join or rejoin.
- **Try again** checks once more (after you joined).
- **Use another Skool account** logs you out of Skool in the app so you can log in with the account that has the membership.

## "No internet connection" or "Can't reach Skool"

- **No internet connection**: connect to the internet, then click **Try again**. The app also needs the internet for Claude to make videos.
- **Can't reach Skool**: you are online, but something blocks skool.com, usually an antivirus (like Bitdefender, Norton or Avast), a firewall or a VPN. Allow Creatorize there, turn the VPN off, then click **Try again**.
- Still stuck? Click **Copy details for support** at the bottom of the window and paste it into a post in the Creatorize community on Skool.

## Your plan

- Your plan (**Standard** or **Premium**) follows your membership in the community. It shows at the bottom of the sidebar.
- After you change your membership on Skool, restart the app to pick it up right away (the app also checks again by itself while it runs).
- If you leave the community, the app locks again and shows the Skool window.

See "Plans: Standard and Premium" for what each plan includes.

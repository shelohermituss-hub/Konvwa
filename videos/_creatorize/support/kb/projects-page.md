# Projects page: open, find, copy and rename projects

platforms: both

The **Projects** page (in Personal Brand: **Videos**) lists every video you made. Click a card to open it in the editor.

## Find a project

- Type in **Search projects** (**Search videos**).
- The sort menu next to the search: **Last edited**, **Name**, **Style** (grouped) or **Date created**. It is remembered.
- Tabs **All**, **Draft**, **Editing**, **Ready** filter by stage (Projects page).
- With channels set up, a channel filter shows one channel. Missing from the list? Click **Show every channel** or **Look in every channel**.
- Still not there? Switch workspace: Faceless Brand projects are not listed in Personal Brand, and the reverse.

## The buttons on a card

- **Show folder**: opens the project's folder in File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac).
- **Duplicate**: a copy named "... (copy)" with its edit history, to try something big safely. Exports are not copied.
- **Harvest now** (the sprout): Claude proposes Library entries from this video.
- **Share as zip**: see "Import and share projects (.zip and folders)".
- **Delete**: see "Delete a project and get it back".

Personal Brand video cards have **Show folder** and **Delete** only.

## Rename a project

Open it, click its name at the top of the editor, type the new name and press Enter.

## "Folder missing"

The folder was moved, renamed or deleted outside the app, or its drive is not connected. The card can't open.

1. To see where it was: click the card's **Delete** button, read the folder path in the dialog, click **Cancel**.
2. Put the folder back there (or connect the drive). Deleted it? Restore it from the Recycle Bin (Windows) or the Trash (Mac).
3. Can't put it back? Use **Import folder** on the Projects page with the folder where it is now, then remove the old card with **Delete > Remove, keep files**.

## "Could not open this project"

Click **Back to projects** and open it again. If it keeps happening, click **Help > Report a problem**.

## More on this page

- **Recent edits** (under the cards): Claude's last edits over all projects. Click one to open that project.
- Projects are folders in your workspace folder: Settings > General > **Workspace** > **Open**.

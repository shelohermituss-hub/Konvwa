# Plans: Standard and Premium
keywords: premium, standard, plan, my plan, which plan, locked, lock icon, premium pill, upgrade, upgrade to premium, export limit, exports left, monthly exports, 12 exports, used this month's exports, exports reset, 4k locked, ultraedit locked, routines locked, youtube locked, analytics locked, style studio locked, characters locked, languages locked, skool, premium feature
platforms: both

Your plan shows at the bottom of the sidebar: **Standard** (with "… of 12 exports left") or **Premium**.

## Standard

- 12 video exports a month. They reset on the 1st.
- Every style and vertical 9:16.
- Random characters, and uploading your own 3D characters.

What counts as an export: a finished full video (the Video export, a full-length vertical 9:16 export, a Personal Brand render). A clip of a range or a trailer doesn't count.

When they are used up you see: "You've used this month's 12 exports on Standard. They reset on the 1st; Premium has unlimited exports."

## Premium adds

- **Ultraedit** (the editor's button and the top quality levels in the Build dialog)
- **Routines**
- Custom styles: **Import style**, **Get the Style Kit**, and new looks with Claude in **the Style Studio**
- **Characters designed with Claude**: Design with Claude, Describe a new character, Change with Claude (uploading your own 3D character in Look > Characters works on every plan)
- **4K export**
- **Other languages** (Several languages, videos not in English)
- **YouTube Analytics** and learning from your channel
- Unlimited exports

## How locks look

Locked pages (Routines, YouTube) show a lock icon in the sidebar. Locked buttons and levels show **Premium** or "(Premium)". Clicking one says "… is a Premium feature. Upgrade to Premium on Skool to unlock it."

## Upgraded but still locked?

Restart the app. Still locked? Click **Help > Report a problem** so it gets fixed.

# Faceless Brand and Personal Brand
keywords: faceless brand, personal brand, switch, brand switch, workspace, switch workspace, change workspace, brand type, toggle, my projects disappeared, projects gone after switch, videos missing, wrong sidebar, different pages, where did my pages go, talking head, explainer, faceless, personal, switch back, menu changed, sidebar changed
platforms: both

Creatorize Suite has two workspaces, each with its own pages and its own list of videos:

- **Faceless Brand**: animated explainer videos that Claude builds in a style (pixel art, Vox, 3D and more).
- **Personal Brand**: editing your own footage (talking head, screen recording) with Claude.

The name under the Creatorize logo shows which one you are in.

## Switch

- Click **Personal Brand** or **Faceless Brand** in the switch at the top of the sidebar (you can also drag it).
- The switch is hidden while the sidebar shows only icons (in the editor, or after collapsing it). Expand the sidebar, or press Ctrl/⌘ + K and pick **Switch to Personal Brand** / **Switch to Faceless Brand**.

The app remembers the workspace you were in for next time.

## What changes

- **The sidebar**: only Faceless Brand has Projects, Styles, Library, Memory and Routines. Only Personal Brand has Videos and Layouts. Home, Channels, Editor, Thumbnails, Renders, YouTube and Settings are in both.
- **The video list**: Projects shows only Faceless Brand projects, Videos only Personal Brand videos.
- **Renders**: each workspace lists its own exports.
- **New project** becomes **New video** (Ctrl/⌘ + N opens the one of the workspace you are in).
- **Editor** reopens the last project of that workspace.

The page you are on stays if the other workspace has it too; otherwise you land on **Home**.

## "My projects are gone"

They are not. You are probably in the other workspace: switch back. Opening a project from a message (its **Open** button) or from a Channels board switches to its workspace by itself.

## Channels hold both

A channel's board shows its Faceless and Personal videos together (see "Channels board").

For how Personal Brand editing works, see "Personal Brand (editing your own footage)".

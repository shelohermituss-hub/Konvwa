# MS Paint stick figures look settings
keywords: ms paint, paint, stick figures, stickman, stick man, drawings, more details, busier pictures, too empty, real images, real photos, wikimedia, photos from the web, real images per minute, no music, music missing, brand colour, labels, red labels, date cards, 9:16, vertical
platforms: both

Change these in the editor: **Look** button > change the setting > **Apply to project**. The style has one look,
**MS Paint**, and is 16:9 only.

- **More details** (off): busier pictures: extra clouds, birds and ground clutter in outdoor backdrops, and Claude
  adds more props and labels to each shot.
- **Use real images from the web** (off): Claude finds real photos on Wikimedia Commons (free, with their licence
  noted) and shows them as framed pictures, like documentary channels do.
- **Real images per minute** (0 to 6, default 2): how many real photos per minute when real images are on; the rest
  stays drawn.
- **Brand colour** (Style default): your channel colour for the labels and the date and number cards instead of red.

**Use real images from the web** is also in New project (**This project's settings** > Real media) and in the editor's
**Project settings**.

## Good to know

- **No music**: this style never has music, only the voice and sound effects. That is on purpose.
- **No grain, vignette or caption settings**: the look has only the settings above.
- The people and animals are drawn once by your picture service and kept, so later videos reuse them. See "Pictures:
  Google Flow, Higgsfield, Fal.ai".

# Low poly shorts characters
keywords: low poly, low-poly, characters, character, main character, hero, cast, who plays, change character, friend, partner, boss, dealer, doctor, stranger, alex, maya, marcus, jen, tom, rosa, frank, lena, automatic, swap character, different person
platforms: both

Low poly shorts star a cast of 8 game-style people. You choose who plays each part in the **Look**: the whole video
switches at once, no scene is edited.

1. Open the video in the editor and click the **Look** button.
2. **Main character**: click a portrait. This is the person "you" are in every scene. Default: **Alex**.
3. **Cast: Friend**, **Cast: Partner**, **Cast: Boss**, **Cast: Dealer**, **Cast: Doctor**, **Cast: Stranger**: click
   a portrait, or **Auto** for the usual person. They keep the role's clothes.
4. Click **Apply to project**.

## The people

- **Alex** (man, leather jacket)
- **Maya** (woman, yellow top)
- **Marcus** (man, hoodie, beard)
- **Jen** (woman, red top, buns)
- **Tom** (man, shirt and tie, glasses)
- **Rosa** (woman, green top)
- **Frank** (older man, sweater)
- **Lena** (woman, blonde, office)

## Good to know

- You can change the cast any time, also after the video is built.
- To make the choice your default for new videos, set it on a copy of a look on the **Styles** page (**Duplicate**,
  pick the people, **Save look**). See "Styles page".
- Uploading your own rigged 3D character is a 3D reconstruction feature (see "3D reconstruction characters").

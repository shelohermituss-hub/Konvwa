# Personal Brand: Claude workflows
keywords: cut the footage, edit the intro, workflows, claude edit footage, remove restarts, false starts, stutters, dead air, bloopers, sync facecam, intro animation, run again, revert the cut, revert the intro, re-apply, ask claude, change, question, apply, ask, stop claude, new conversation, opus 5.5, sonnet 5.5, how long does the cut take, add the screen recording first, sign in to claude
platforms: both

Claude edits Personal Brand videos with two workflows in the **Workflows** tab (left side of the editor), and answers or changes things in the **Claude** tab (right side).

## Cut the footage
Keeps only the sentences your video needs, each from your last clean take: restarts, stutters, breaths and dead air are left out. It syncs your facecam clips into one, and every cut is checked before it reaches your timeline. Usually 20-45 minutes.
- Needs a file marked **Screen recording** that has sound. "Add the screen recording first" / "The screen recording has no audio": fix it in **Footage**.

## Edit the intro
Animated text and graphics on your intro read, then sound effects on every move. The edited intro goes to the start of the timeline. Usually 15-30 minutes.
- Needs a file marked **Intro** in **Footage**.

## Start one
1. **Workflows** > click the workflow.
2. **Notes (optional)**, e.g. "keep the long demo at 12:00".
3. Pick the model: **Opus 5.5** (the most careful edit) or **Sonnet 5.5** (faster, lighter on your plan).
4. **Start** (**Run again** after it ran once).

Progress shows in the **Claude** tab; **Stop** ends it. When it is done, watch it through on the timeline.

## Undo it
The result card in the Claude tab has **Revert the cut** / **Revert the intro**: the timeline goes back to how it was. **Re-apply** brings it back.

## Ask Claude
At the bottom of the **Claude** tab:
- **Change**, then **Apply**: e.g. "Tighten the pauses a little more", "Put back the part about the setup".
- **Question**, then **Ask**: e.g. "What did you cut, and why?", "Write chapters for YouTube".
- **New conversation** (+): Claude starts fresh; the edits stay.
- When Claude wants to do something that needs your OK, it asks: **Allow** or **Deny**.

## Problems
- **Sign in to Claude** shows: Claude is not connected (see "Claude not installed or not signed in").
- "Claude is still working": wait, or **Stop** it first.
- "Claude stopped before the cut was delivered": ask it to finish, or start **Cut the footage** again.

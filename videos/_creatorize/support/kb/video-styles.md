A style is the kind of video: what gets drawn and how. You pick it in **New project**; the **Styles** page lists them all.

- **Pixel art**: 3D scenes drawn as crisp low-resolution pixel art with a retro-font overlay. 16:9 only.
- **Vox**: newspaper cut-out collage explainers on one front page. Its pictures are made by your picture
  service (see "Image generation: Google Flow, Higgsfield, Fal.ai"). 16:9 or 9:16.
- **Flat explainer**: bright flat-vector explainers starring your own main character. No pictures needed. 9:16 or 16:9.
- **MS Paint stick figures**: funny history and science explainers drawn like MS Paint, a new picture every 2-3
  seconds. They never have music. 16:9 only.
- **3D reconstruction**: documentaries of real events with real maps, photos, footage and free 3D models. Two looks:
  **Studio** (bright clay) and **Investigation** (dark and cinematic, for any topic). 16:9 or 9:16.
- **Low poly shorts**: fast low-poly 3D shorts with blocky game characters, looks **Bright** or **Moody**. 9:16 or 16:9.
- **3D cartoon shorts**: big-headed 3D cartoon characters with faces that change on the words, in busy dressed sets.
  Two engines: **Three.js** (live in the app) or **Blender** (richer light, longer exports; see "3D cartoon shorts and
  the Blender engine"). 9:16 or 16:9.
- **3D Informational (Zack D Like)**: realistic 3D explainers rendered in Blender: a big "what if" idea built step by
  step, the named part glowing green, a camera that never stops (see "3D Informational"). 9:16 or 16:9.
- **Stick stories**: "What if you..." stories acted by black stick people in illustrated sets. 16:9 or 9:16.
- **Your styles**: styles you built with the Style Kit and imported, under **Custom styles** in New project (see "Custom styles and the Style Kit").

**Vox editorial (old)** still opens old projects but can't be picked for new ones.

## Style vs look

A **style** is what gets drawn. A **look** is the settings on top: colours, grain, camera, captions. Change the look of a
video any time with the **Look** button in the editor (see "Look settings"). Each style has its own article listing
every setting, e.g. "Pixel art look settings".

## Can I change the style of a video?

There is no button to switch an existing video to another style: the scenes are built for their style. Make a new
project in the other style and paste the same script (**I have a script**) or reuse the same voiceover.

## Can I change 16:9 / 9:16 later?

No, the format is set when the project is made. See "Wide 16:9 or vertical 9:16".

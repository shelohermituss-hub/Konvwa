# Getting started with Creatorize Suite
keywords: start, first video, beginner, how to use, new, new member, tutorial, where do i begin, where to start, overview, getting started, first time, first run, just installed, setup, set up, onboarding, what now, what do i do, log in with skool, skool login, welcome, checklist, how does it work, step by step, first project
platforms: both

Creatorize Suite makes videos with your own Claude Code. You watch the video live, click what you want changed, say it in words, and Claude edits it. Every edit can be undone.

## When the app opens

1. The app checks your Skool membership. If it asks, click **Log in with Skool** and log in with your Skool account.
2. "No internet connection" or "Can't reach Skool": connect (or allow the app in your antivirus, firewall or VPN), then click **Try again**. More in "Logging in with Skool (membership)".
3. **Home** opens with a **Getting started** list. Work through it:
   - **Connect Claude**: click **Sign in** (see "Claude not installed or not signed in").
   - **Check your tools**: git, ffmpeg and Python. **Review** opens Settings if one is missing.
   - **Set up transcription**: Claude installs a speech model on this computer. It starts by itself once Claude is connected.
   - **Create or import a project**, **Make your first edit**, **Export a video**.

## Your first video

1. Click **New project** at the bottom of the sidebar (Ctrl/⌘ + N).
2. Name the video, pick a **Style**, a **Look** (some styles: **Format**) and a **Length**. Add a voiceover now or later.
3. Under **Story**: **Claude writes it** (type a topic) or **I have a script** (paste it).
4. Click **Create project**. The editor opens.
5. Work down the **Pipeline** on the left: Script written, Voiceover added, Audio cleaned, Words timed, Scenes built, Sound design mixed, Video exported. Each step has its button (**Write**, **Add**, **Run**, **Build**, **Mix**, **Export**).
6. When the scenes are built, click anything in the preview and tell Claude what to change.
7. Click **Export** at the top when you are happy.

## Two workspaces

The switch at the top of the sidebar picks **Faceless Brand** (animated videos made by Claude) or **Personal Brand** (editing your own footage). See "Faceless Brand and Personal Brand".

## Finding things fast

- Ctrl/⌘ + K (or **Search or run…** in the sidebar) jumps to any page, project or setting.
- **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar answers questions. If something is broken, click **Help > Report a problem**.

A layout is how a Personal Brand video is framed: the screen recording, your facecam and the background behind them.

## The Layouts page
Personal Brand sidebar > Studio > **Layouts**.
- Built-in: **Classic**, **Big facecam**, **Screen focus**. **Copy and change** makes your own copy.
- **New layout** starts from the default one. Your own layouts have **Edit** and **Delete** (videos already using one keep their look).
- **Make default**: new videos start with it (it shows **Default** with a star). Each video can still change its own.
- **Backgrounds**: **Add your own** (JPG, PNG or WebP). Then pick it in any layout.

## In the editor
The **Layout** tab (left side of the Personal editor) changes this video only:
- Drag the boxes in the preview to move them; drag a corner to resize.
- **Layout presets**: click one to apply it (the starred one is the default). **Save as preset** keeps the current one under a name; the message after it has **Make default**.
- **Background**: pick an image (or add yours), **Dim**, **Blur**.
- **Screen recording**: **Size**, **Corners**, **Shadow**, **Fill the box** (crop instead of fit).
- **Facecam**: **Show** (switch it off for screen only), **Size**, **Corners**, **Shadow**, **Zoom**, **Corner** (top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right).

The layout is used in the preview and in the final render.

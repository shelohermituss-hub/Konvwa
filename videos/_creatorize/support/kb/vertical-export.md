# Vertical 9:16 versions and trailers
keywords: vertical, 9:16, portrait, shorts, youtube shorts, tiktok, reels, instagram, phone version, crop, reframe, reframed version, vertical cut, trailer, teaser, clip, 30 seconds, 60 seconds, blurred background, black bars, text cut off, export vertical, export trailer, 1080x1920, captions on shorts
platforms: both

Open **Export** > **Vertical 9:16**. The phone on the right shows the result (its arrows step through sample shots).

## Made from

- **Vertical cut**: the 16:9 video turned tall. Quick, no Claude needed.
  - **Fill the frame**: **Crop to fill** keeps a third of the picture, enlarged. **Fit with blurred background** shows the whole picture on a blurred copy of itself. Wide titles get cut with Crop: use Fit or the Reframed version.
  - **Position** (Crop only): the slider from Left to Right, or **Centre**. You can also drag the picture on the phone (double-click centres it). One position is used for the whole video.
- **Reframed version**: click **Make the 9:16 version**. Claude copies the project to "<title> (9:16)" (same voiceover, timing and sound) and lays every scene out again for the tall frame. It runs on your Claude plan and takes about as long as a build; your video stays as it is. Keep working meanwhile, then export it here, or **Open the 9:16 project** to fine-tune shots. Reframing works for Flat explainer and Vox videos (and some of your own styles); for other styles the window says "<style> can't be reframed": use the vertical cut.

A project made vertical from the start (**Format** in New project) skips this.

## Length

- **Full video**.
- **Trailer** (Shorts): pick the shot it **Starts at** and **Up to** 30 s, 45 s or 60 s. It ends where a shot or a sentence ends. Only that part is rendered.
- **Selected**: the range selected on the timeline (shows when one is selected).

## Captions and end card

- **Captions**: big word-by-word captions in the lower part, from the timed words (or the video's own captions if its look has them). Needs **Words timed**.
- **End card**: 3.5 s at the end. See "Vertical end card".

## Export

The button says **Export vertical**, **Export trailer** or **Export clip**. You get a 1080×1920 MP4 named ..._vertical, ..._trailer or ..._clip in the out folder; it doesn't tick **Video exported**. On Standard a full-length vertical video counts as one monthly export; trailers and clips don't.

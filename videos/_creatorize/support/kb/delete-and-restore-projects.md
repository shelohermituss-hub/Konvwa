# Delete a project and get it back
keywords: delete, delete project, remove project, delete video, deleted by accident, accidentally deleted, undo delete, restore project, recover project, get project back, bring back, trash, recycle bin, remove keep files, move to trash, move to recycle bin, free space, disk space, clean up, can't delete, won't delete, folder still in use, permanently delete
platforms: both

Deleting takes a project out of the app. The app never deletes its folder for good: the folder is kept, or goes to the Recycle Bin (Windows) or the Trash (Mac).

## Delete

1. On the **Projects** page, click the card's **Delete** button (the bin icon).
2. The dialog shows the project's folder. Pick:
   - **Remove, keep files**: gone from the app, the folder stays where it is.
   - **Move to Recycle Bin** (Mac: **Move to Trash**): gone from the app, the folder goes to the bin.

In Personal Brand (**Videos** page) the buttons are **Remove, keep folder** and **Move project to Recycle Bin** (Mac: **Trash**). Your footage is never deleted: it stays where it is.

Also gone with the project: its own Memory rules (the **One project** rules). There is no undo button in the app.

## Get a deleted project back (Faceless Brand)

1. Moved to the bin? Restore the folder first:
   - On Windows: open the Recycle Bin, right-click the folder, **Restore**.
   - On Mac: open the Trash, right-click the folder, **Put Back**.
2. In the app: **Projects** > **Import folder** and pick that folder. It is in your workspace folder (Settings > General > **Workspace** > **Open**), in its projects folder.
3. The app opens it as a copy. You can then delete the folder you picked.

It comes back without its old edit history and without its own Memory rules (add them again on **Memory**).

Emptied the bin already? Then the app can't bring it back.

## Personal Brand

There is no import in Personal Brand, so a removed video can't come back into **Videos**. Your footage is untouched: plan it again with **New video**.

## "Can't delete" messages

- "Claude is working on this project. Stop it first, then delete.": press Stop in the Claude panel, or wait.
- "This video is being exported...": wait for the export, or cancel it in its Export dialog.
- "...its folder is still in use...": the project left the app, but a player or another program has a file open. Close it, then delete the folder yourself.

# Your own music and levels
keywords: own music, my music, add music, upload music, import music, background music, music track, song, replace music, remove music, music level, music volume, music too loud, music too quiet, ducking, duck under voice, music drowns the voice, voice too quiet, effects level, sound effects too loud, style's music, mp3 music, loop music
platforms: both

## Add your own music

Open **Project settings** (the gear next to the video's name) or Pipeline > **Music & effects** > **Layers** (at the top), under **Your own music**.

1. Click **Add your own music...** and pick a file (mp3, wav, m4a, ogg, flac, aac or opus).
2. It plays under the whole video: looped or cut to the length, faded in and out, and turned down while someone speaks.
3. **Mine** uses your track. **Off (the style's own sound)** goes back to the style's music. **Replace...** and **Remove** change it any time.

## Levels

- **Music level** (your track): -40 dB (off) to 0 dB.
- **Duck under the voice**: how far your music drops while someone speaks, from "no ducking" to -11 dB.
- **Effects level**: Pipeline > **Music & effects** > **Auto sound design** > **Sound effects** (**On** / **Off**), -12 to +6 dB ("normal" at 0).
- In **Layers**, each lane has a volume slider, and each layer its own **Volume** and **Duck under voice**.

The style's own music has no level slider. Too loud? Use your own music with your own level, or switch **Music** off for this video in **Project settings > Sound**.

## Hearing the change

Changes are mixed again within seconds: play the preview. If the app says "Press Redo on "Sound design mixed" to hear the change", click **Redo** on that step in the Pipeline.

Then export again: an earlier export keeps its old sound.

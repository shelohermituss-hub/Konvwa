# The pipeline steps
keywords: pipeline, steps, workflow, order, checklist, what next, build, build scenes, scenes built, script written, voiceover added, audio cleaned, words timed, storyboard, sound design mixed, video exported, greyed out, disabled button, can't click, outdated, redo, set up, needs, pipeline missing, where is the pipeline, clean and time, time words
platforms: both

The **Pipeline** on the left of the editor shows where a video is. A tick means done.

1. **Script written**: **Write** (Claude writes it), later **Open**.
2. **Voiceover added**: **Add**, later **Replace**.
3. **Audio cleaned**: **Run**, later **Redo**.
4. **Words timed**: **Run**, later **Redo**. Claude builds the scenes to these timings.
5. **Storyboard written**: Claude writes it during the build (no button).
6. **Scenes built**: **Build**, later **Redo**.
7. **Sound design mixed**: **Mix**, later **Redo**.
8. **Video exported**: **Export**.

Below the steps: **Trim silences** (once the audio is cleaned), **Music & effects** and **Ultraedit**.

## Why is a button greyed out?

Each step needs the one before it: hover over the step to see "Needs: …". Also:
- "Wait for the voiceover to finish": a voice is being made.
- "Claude is working on this project: wait until it has finished".
- Before the words are timed, **Scenes built** shows **Set up**: give your direction now, the build starts later.

## The bar under the preview

After a new voiceover a bar says it isn't cleaned and timed yet. Click **Clean and time** (or **Time words**). Until then the scenes and an export still follow the previous voiceover.

## "outdated"

- On **Sound design mixed**: the scenes changed after the mix. Click **Redo**.
- On **Video exported**: the narration or its sound changed after the export. Export again.

## Can't see the Pipeline?

It hides when the window is narrower than about 1350 pixels; Ctrl/⌘ + B also hides and shows it. Make the window wider. The **Voiceover** button (sound-wave icon) and **Export** stay at the top.

## Ultraedit (Premium)

Select shots in the shot list or on the timeline (Shift + click for more), then click **Ultraedit**: Claude upgrades them one by one, with a before and after for each.

# Music, sound effects and the mix
keywords: sound, audio, music, sound effects, sfx, mix, mixing, sound design, too loud, too quiet, music too loud, can't hear the voice, no music, no sound effects, turn off music, mute music, background music, ambience, whoosh, sound design mixed, mix step, remix, levels, sound settings
platforms: both

A video's sound has three parts: the voice, the automatic sound design (music and ambience beds, effects on the picture's events), and sounds you place yourself.

## Where to change what

- **Every video**: **Settings > Media > Sound**: **Music** (music and ambience beds) and **Sound effects** (sounds on cuts, cards and accents), on or off. MS Paint stick figures never have music.
- **This video**: **Project settings** (the gear next to the video's name) > **Sound**: **Music** and **Sound effects**, each **Global setting**, **On** or **Off**. A change applies at the next Mix.
- **Your own music, levels and ducking**: see "Your own music and levels".
- **Sounds you place yourself**, your sound library, which effects the sound design may use: Pipeline > **Music & effects**. See "Sound effects and audio layers".

## The Mix step

**Sound design mixed** in the Pipeline (**Mix**, later **Redo**) makes the automatic sound design and mixes it with the voice. Run it again after:
- switching Music or Sound effects on or off,
- the scenes changed (the step says "outdated"),
- a new voiceover or **Trim silences**.

Layers you place and your own music are mixed in within seconds, without Claude: play the preview to hear them. If the app says "Press Redo on "Sound design mixed" to hear the change", do that.

## Then export again

An export keeps the sound it was made with. Export again after a sound change.

**No music in the export?** The export window says what goes in. "the clean voiceover (no sound design yet)" means the mix is missing or older than the voiceover: run **Sound design mixed** first.

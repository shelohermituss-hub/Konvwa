# YouTube analytics and channel sign-in

platforms: both

The **YouTube** page (sidebar, under Analytics; Premium) shows how your videos do. To connect a channel see "YouTube page: connect your channels".

## The top of the page
- The channel picker (when you follow several), **Refresh** (reads YouTube again), open on YouTube, **+** to follow another channel.
- The **…** menu: **Stop using the Google sign-in…** (keeps public stats) and **Stop following…** (the app forgets its numbers; nothing changes on YouTube).

## The numbers
- **7 days** / **28 days** / **90 days**, compared with the period before.
- Signed in: **Views**, **Watch hours**, **Subscribers** (gained minus lost), **Avg view duration**, and a chart per day.
- Public stats: **Subscribers**, **Views, all time**, **Videos**, **Likes on recent videos**. Growth shows once the app has read the channel on a second day.

## Videos
Sort, filter (**Public**, **All videos**, **Private and unlisted**) and search the **Videos** table. Click a video for its **Audience retention**, **Where viewers came from** and **Comments**. The **Project** column links a video to its project (see "Channels: stages, Auto and By hand").

## Good to know
- YouTube's analytics lag about two days; retention shows once enough people watched.
- Impressions and click-through rate are not shown here: see them in YouTube Studio.

## Problems
- **Analytics are not allowed for this channel**: when you signed in, the YouTube Analytics box was not ticked. **Sign in again** and tick both boxes.
- **Google ended the sign-in for this channel**: click **Sign in again**. If it happens every 7 days, your Google app is still in Testing: click **Publish app** (guide step 4), then sign in again. **Public stats only** keeps the channel without the sign-in.
- **The last read did not work** / **Not everything could be read**: the note says why; **Refresh** tries again.
- "Comments need a free Google API key (Settings > YouTube)": see "YouTube: comments and What's working".

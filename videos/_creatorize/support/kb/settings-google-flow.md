# Google Flow for pictures (free with your Flow credits)
keywords: google flow, flow, flow credits, free pictures, nano banana, nano banana 2.1, chrome, google chrome, needs google chrome, open flow to sign in, flow test, flow window, flow not working, flow stopped, flow closed, google sign in, gemini pictures, chrome window closed, flow prompt box, at your own risk
platforms: both

Google Flow makes your pictures with your own Flow credits (Nano Banana 2.1), in a Chrome window of its own. It is in **Settings > Media > Image generation**, under **Google Flow**. It is at your own risk: not supported, and if Flow changes its page it may stop working.

## Set it up

1. Install Google Chrome if you don't have it (free from google.com/chrome). Without it the row says "Needs Google Chrome".
2. Click **Open Flow to sign in**. A Chrome window opens: sign in to your Google account there.
3. Click **Test**. It makes one small test picture on your Flow account and shows it.
4. Choose how it is used, in **Pictures in Vox and custom styles**:
   - **Automatic** (default) uses Flow first and switches to Higgsfield or Fal.ai (if you added a key) when Flow can't make a picture.
   - **Google Flow (at your own risk)** uses only Flow and never a paid service.

## While a video builds

- Keep that Chrome window open. Pictures are made one at a time. It can stay behind other windows, but don't minimize it: Flow stops while it's minimized (Creatorize opens it again by itself). A picture that takes over 8 minutes is stopped with a message.
- If Flow asks you to sign in again, do it in that window.

## Problems

- "Google Chrome is not installed": install Chrome (On Mac: put it in Applications), then try again.
- "Google Flow is picked but not set up on this computer yet": click **Open Flow to sign in**, sign in, then try again.
- "The Flow Chrome window was closed": click **Open Flow to sign in** again and keep the window open.
- "Chrome started but did not answer": close any Flow Chrome window that is already open and try again.
- "Flow did not show its prompt box": in the Flow window, make sure you are signed in and a project is open, then try again.
- "The aspect ratio can't be set" during setup: Creatorize switches Flow's Agent mode off by itself (it hides the aspect-ratio setting). If setup still says the aspect ratio can't be set, look at the bar under the prompt in the Flow window: if it only shows Agent, click Agent so it's grey, then **Test** again.
- "Flow may have changed its page": Flow changed; pick Higgsfield or Fal.ai until it works again.

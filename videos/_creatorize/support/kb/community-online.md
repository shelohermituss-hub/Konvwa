# Showing as online in the community
keywords: online, show me online, green dot, offline, appear online, active, presence, community, skool, status, background, other members see me, turn off online, hide online, privacy, skool tab
platforms: both

While Creatorize is open, the Creatorize community on Skool can stay open in the background, like a tab you left open in your browser. Other members then see you as online in the community.

## Turn it on or off

1. Open **Settings** (bottom of the sidebar).
2. On the **General** tab, find **Show me online in the community**.
3. Switch **Show me online** on or off. It takes effect right away.

It is on by default.

## What it does and doesn't do

- It only keeps the community page open with your Skool sign-in. Nothing is posted, liked or sent for you.
- When it is off, the app still checks your membership about every half hour, but only opens Skool for a moment.
- It ends when you close Creatorize.

## Opening the app

If you were checked as a member within the last day, Creatorize opens straight away without the **Checking your Skool membership...** window, and checks in the background. If you left the community in the meantime, the app locks and shows **Not a member**.

# Channels board

The **Channels** page (sidebar, in both workspaces) shows your videos and ideas on a board, from idea to published, grouped by channel.

## The page
- Left: **All channels**, your channels, and **No channel**. **New channel** makes one (see "Channels: make, edit and link a channel").
- **All channels** shows a tile per channel (in progress, due this week, ready, out this month). Click a tile to open its board.
- Eight stages: Idea, Script, Voiceover, Building, Review, Ready, Scheduled, Published (see "Channels: stages, Auto and By hand").
- **New project** (Faceless Brand) or **New video** (Personal Brand), top right, starts one in the channel shown.

## Tools above the board
Search; **All** / **Faceless** / **Personal**; **Due this week** (due in the next 7 days, or late); the stage picker (**All stages** or one); compact cards or cards with pictures. When filters hide cards, click **Show everything**.

## Cards
- Click a card to open it. Double-click its title to rename it.
- Right-click a card (or open its menu): **Move to stage**, **Move to channel**, **Due date…**, **Plan note…**, **YouTube video…**, **Open**, **Rename**, and **Show in Explorer** (Windows) / **Show in Finder** (Mac).
- Drag a card to another stage, or onto a channel in the left list to move it there.
- Pick several: Ctrl/⌘ + click, Shift + click for a range, Ctrl/⌘ + A for every card shown. The bar at the bottom moves them all: **Move to stage**, **Move to channel**, **Due date**. Esc unpicks.

## Problems
- **Folder missing** on a card: the project folder was moved, renamed, or its drive is unplugged. Put it back or connect the drive (see "Projects page: open, find, copy and rename projects").
- **Its files could not be read**: something in its folder is not what the app expects, or the app has no permission to read it.
- **The board could not be read**: click **Try again**.

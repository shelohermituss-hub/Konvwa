# Memory: rules Claude always follows
keywords: memory, memory page, rules, style rules, add rule, new rule, claude keeps doing, claude keeps forgetting, always, never, remember, remember this, preferences, every video, one project, global rule, project rule, suggested rules, suggested by claude, learn from my edits, keep, dismiss, turn off rule, delete rule, claude ignores my rule, stop doing
platforms: both

**Memory** (Faceless Brand, under **Workspace**) holds short rules Claude follows every time, like camera framing, text placement or colour choices. Use it for things you would otherwise say in every edit.

## Add a rule

1. Open **Memory**.
2. Type it under **New rule**, for example "Never place the camera behind the character being talked about".
3. Pick where it applies: **All videos**, **One project** (then pick the project), **One style** or **One channel**. A style or channel rule applies to every project in that style or on that channel.
4. Click **Add rule**. Claude gets it with your next request.

Short and concrete works best: one preference per rule.

## Manage rules

- Tabs: **All**, **Global** (all videos), **Project** (one project), **Suggested**.
- The switch on a rule turns it off without deleting it.
- The bin icon deletes it.

## Rules Claude suggests

- **Learn from my edits** (top right): Claude reads your recent edit requests and suggests rules for what you keep asking for.
- Claude can also suggest one by itself when an edit shows a preference. A message pops up with **Review**, and **new** shows next to **Memory** in the sidebar.
- Suggested rules wait under **Suggested**, marked "Suggested by Claude". Click **Keep** to use it, or **Dismiss**.

A suggestion does nothing until you click **Keep**.

## Good to know

- A **One project** rule is copied with **Duplicate**, and deleted when that project is deleted.
- Claude still not following a rule? Make it shorter and more exact, and check that its switch is on and it is in the right scope.
- Memory is for rules. Reusable scenes, models and fixes are in the **Library** (see "Library: what Claude reuses between videos").

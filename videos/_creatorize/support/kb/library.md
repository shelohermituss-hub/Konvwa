# Library: what Claude reuses between videos
keywords: library, library page, reuse, reusable, harvest, harvest now, grow the library, proposed, proposals, accept, reject, review, inbox, new entries, scene recipe, models, materials, fixes, know-how, pitfall, verify, flag, retire, bring back, deleted entries, move deleted to trash, recycle bin, built-in, faster builds, same mistake again
platforms: both

The **Library** (Faceless Brand, under **Workspace**) holds what Claude can reuse instead of building it again: 3D models, code, materials, scene recipes and the fixes it learned. Claude searches it before it builds, so every video makes the next one faster.

## Find things

- Search box: try words like glow, brick wall, flicker, chair.
- Tabs **All**, **Assets**, **Scenes**, **Know-how**.
- Filters on the left: **Source** (Mine, Built-in, Low poly assets, Sounds, Characters, Sprites, 3D figures, Pictures), **Kind**, **Style**, **Where it applies**, **Status**. **Clear filters** resets them.
- Click a card to open the whole entry.

## New entries from Claude

Claude only reuses entries you accept.

1. After a build (with **Grow the library after builds** on, the default), Claude looks back over the video and proposes entries. **new** shows next to **Library** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Review** on the banner ("... proposed entries wait for you"), or use **Accept** / **Reject** under **From the last harvest**.
3. In an open proposed entry: **Accept** or **Delete**.

To propose from one video yourself: pick it next to **Harvest now** at the top and click it (also the sprout button on a Projects card). Turn the automatic look-back on or off in Settings > AI engines > **Grow the library after builds**.

## On an entry

- **Verify**: it worked in a real video.
- **Flag**: something is wrong with it. Claude stops reusing it.
- **Retire**: keep it, but Claude no longer uses it. **Bring back** undoes that.
- **Edit**: title, summary, tags (the words Claude searches with) and **Where Claude may use it**.
- **Delete**, and **Show in Explorer** (Mac: **Show in Finder**).

Built-in entries come with the app and are read-only. Entries from your sounds, characters and other libraries are read-only here too.

## Deleted entries

A deleted entry waits in the library's deleted items (the message has **Undo**). **Move ... deleted to the Recycle Bin** (Mac: **Trash**) at the top clears them out.

Rules Claude must always follow belong in **Memory** (see "Memory: rules Claude always follows").

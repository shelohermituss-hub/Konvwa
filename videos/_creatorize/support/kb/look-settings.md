# Look settings (grain, colours, captions, camera)

platforms: both

The look is the finish of a video: colours, grain, vignette, captions, camera moves. Every style has its own settings
(see the style's own article, e.g. "Vox look settings").

## Change the look of one video

1. Open the project in the editor.
2. Click the **Look** button in the top bar. It shows the style and the look name, e.g. "Vox · Press".
3. Click a look to preview it on the current frame (**Now** and **With these settings** side by side), or move the
   settings below. **Preview** shows your changes on the frame.
4. Click **Apply to project**. The whole video changes at once (every shot); your scenes are untouched.

When you switch looks, the video keeps its **Brand colour** (if the new look has none), its 3D reconstruction
**Characters** and its Graphics settings.

## Where is the setting I need?

- **Grain / noise**: **Film grain** (Vox, Flat explainer, 3D reconstruction). Low poly shorts and Pixel art have no
  grain in the look.
- **Dark corners**: **Vignette** (every style except MS Paint stick figures). Lower it or set it to 0.
- **Captions**: **Captions**, **Caption size** (Flat explainer, Low poly shorts), **Caption height** (Low poly shorts).
- **Shaky camera**: **Camera shake** (Low poly shorts). Slow zoom on every shot: **Camera push-in** or **Camera creep
  per shot**.
- **Outlines**: **Depth outlines**, **Object outlines**, **Outline colour** (Pixel art).
- **Your channel colour**: **Brand colour** (every style). **Style default** puts the style's own colours back.

## Quick fixes for one video

Click **Graphics** in the editor top bar and search (e.g. "grain"). It changes the video live and shows only what the
style supports. **Reset to look** puts it back. See "Graphics panel".

## Save a look for later

On the **Styles** page pick a look, click **Duplicate**, change your copy and click **Save look**. Built-in looks are
read-only. Or let Claude make one with **New look** (Style Studio, Premium). See "Styles page".

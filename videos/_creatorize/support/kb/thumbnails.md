The **Thumbnails** page (Faceless Brand: under Workspace; Personal Brand: under Studio) is where you make thumbnails from AI pictures, your own images and text.

## Boards
- Click the grid button next to the board name to switch boards. The **Master** board is always there and can't be deleted.
- Each video or project can have its own board: pick it, or **Start its board**. **New board** makes an empty one.
- Click the board name to rename it. The bin button deletes a board (confirm with **Delete**); its images go to the Recycle Bin (Windows) or Trash (Mac).
- **For <name>** at the top: **Use as thumbnail** puts the image straight on that video or project. The **×** stops it.

## Canvas or Prompt
Switch at the top: **Canvas** (everything on one board you can arrange) or **Prompt** (one simple prompt field). Both use the same board. See "Thumbnails: prompts and models".

## On the canvas
- **Image**: add images (or drop or paste them).
- **Prompt** (P): a new prompt.
- **Text** (T, or double-click the canvas): bold, bigger or smaller, drag the side to set the width (it wraps), **Auto width** for one line.
- Fit everything: Shift + 1. Zoom: + / - or Shift + 0 for 100 %. Hold Space and drag to move around.
- Ctrl/⌘ + Z undo, Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Z redo, Ctrl/⌘ + D duplicate, Delete removes.

## On an image
**Use as thumbnail** (pick the video or project), **Save a copy**, **Show in folder**, and **Open big** (double-click). Open big shows the images large; the arrows go to the next one, Esc closes.

## Messages
- "N images are in this board's folder but not on the canvas": **Put them back** or **Dismiss**.
- The key button says **Add Higgsfield key**: pictures on this page are made on Higgsfield with your own key. Click it, paste the key from cloud.higgsfield.ai (both parts, KEY_ID:KEY_SECRET), **Save key**. **Get a key** opens their site.
- "A connected image is still generating": wait for it, or disconnect it.

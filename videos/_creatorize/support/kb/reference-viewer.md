# Reference viewer: pick the parts of a video for Claude
keywords: reference viewer, pick parts, parts to look at, range, time range, add a range, set in, set out, in point, out point, loop range, preview extract, what claude sees, contact sheet, long video, only part of the video, specific seconds, timestamps, first 20 seconds, note for claude, label, reference video, reference audio, view reference, zoom image, j k l
platforms: both

The reference viewer opens when you click **Pick parts** (a video or sound file) or **View** (a picture or PDF) on a card in the **References** window, or click a reference chip under your text. It picks which seconds Claude looks at. For adding and mentioning references, see "References: show Claude your own files".

## Pick the parts of a video or sound

1. Play it (Space) to find the part you want.
2. Mark a part: drag the handles on the timeline, type the start and end times, or press **Set in** (I) and **Set out** (O) at the playhead.
3. **Add a range at ...** adds another part at the playhead. Name each range if you like.
4. **Loop range** replays the selected part.

Saved parts are listed under **Parts to look at**. With nothing picked, Claude looks at the first 20 seconds. A time range typed in your text, like @vid1[1:02:00-1:02:20], shows here too: **Save as a range** keeps it.

**Insert** on a range puts that exact mention in your text.

## Check what Claude will see

Click **Preview extract** under **What Claude sees**. It reads only the picked seconds, so it is quick even for long videos, and shows the frames (and the words, when there is speech). **Stop** cancels it.

## Tell Claude what to take from it

- **Label**: a name for the reference.
- **Note for Claude**: for example "Copy the camera moves and pacing, not the people" or "Match this colour palette and lighting".

## Keys

Space play or pause, J / K / L back 2 s / pause / play, Left and Right arrow one frame, Shift + arrow 1 s.

## Pictures and PDFs

- A picture: click it for full size, click again to fit. Ctrl/⌘ + scroll zooms.
- A PDF: **Open** shows it in its own app. Claude reads it from where it is.

## "... is not where it was"

The file was moved, renamed or is on a drive that is not connected. Click **Relink…** and pick the file where it is now: its tag, label and ranges stay.

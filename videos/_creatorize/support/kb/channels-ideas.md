# Channels: ideas, due dates and Coming up
keywords: idea, ideas, add an idea, video ideas, idea list, paste ideas, start a faceless project, start a personal video, turn idea into project, edit idea, delete idea, undo delete idea, due date, deadline, late, overdue, due this week, plan note, note on card, coming up, home, reminder, schedule videos, calendar
platforms: both

The **Idea** stage of a channel board holds videos you have not started yet. Any card can get a due date and a plan note.

## Add ideas
- Type in **Add an idea…** at the top of the Idea stage and press Enter.
- Paste a list there: one idea per line.
- Click an idea to open it: the title, **Notes** (they become the topic Claude researches, and the plan note), **Channel**, **Stage** and **Due**. **Save**, or **Start project** (Faceless Brand) / **Start video** (Personal Brand) to save it and open the new project dialog with it filled in.

## Idea card menu
Right-click an idea: **Edit idea…**, **Rename**, **Start a Faceless project**, **Start a Personal video**, **Move to stage**, **Move to channel**, **Due date…**, **Delete idea**. With ideas picked (Ctrl/⌘ + click), the Delete key (Windows) or ⌘⌫ (Mac) deletes them. The message that follows has **Undo**.

## Due dates
1. Card menu > **Due date…** (or **Due date** on the bar when several cards are picked).
2. Click **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **In 3 days**, **In a week** or **In 2 weeks**, or type a date under **Or a date** and **Save**.
3. **No due date** takes it away.

Cards show when they are due or late. **Due this week** above the board shows only those.

## Plan notes
Card menu > **Plan note…** (or **Edit plan note…**): what is left to do, the angle, a reminder. It shows on the card. Ctrl/⌘ + Enter saves.

## Coming up on Home
**Home** shows **Coming up** when something is due in the next 7 days or late, across every channel. Click a row to open it, **Open the board** for the board, or the "more due" line to see them all there.

Routines can take a channel's next idea cards as topics with the **Channel ideas** node (see "Routine nodes: inputs").

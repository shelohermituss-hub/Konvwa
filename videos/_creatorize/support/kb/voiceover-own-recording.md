# Voiceover from your own recording
keywords: my recording, own voice, my voice, record myself, recorded narration, upload voiceover, upload audio, import audio, add audio file, mp3, wav, m4a, video file, microphone, several files, multiple recordings, join recordings, order of recordings, pause between, recording not working, not audio, could not be read, no sound in it, timing wrong, words not heard
platforms: both

1. Open the Voiceover window (**Voiceover added > Add**, or **Replace**) and pick **My recording**.
2. Click **Choose your recording…**, or drop the file anywhere in the window. WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG, AIFF, WMA and more, or a video file.
3. Recorded in parts? Add more files: they are joined in the order shown. Drag a row or use the up and down arrows to reorder it; **X** removes it. Choose the pause between them (none up to 2 s). Up to 500 files.
4. Click **Use this recording** (or **Join N recordings**).

Keep **Then clean the audio and time the words** ticked: the app cleans the audio and times your words so Claude can build the scenes to them.

## Good to know

- The app doesn't record: record in any app you like, then add the file.
- The app keeps its own copy, so your file can stay where it is.
- Your words are timed by transcription on this computer. Not set up yet? See "Transcription setup (word timing)".
- Read the script as written: words that aren't heard get guessed times ("Timed, but N of M words of the script were not heard").
- No script? A recording works without one.

## Messages

- "… is not audio or video" or "… could not be read": the file is damaged or not really audio.
- "… no sound in it": the file is silent.
- "ffmpeg was not found, so the recordings can't be read": see "System check and missing tools".

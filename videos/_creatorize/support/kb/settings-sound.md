# Sound settings: music and sound effects on or off
keywords: sound, sound settings, music, background music, no music, turn off music, music off, sound effects, sfx, no sound effects, turn off sound effects, ambience, ambience beds, audio, mix, mix step, sound design, too much music, music too loud, stick figures no music, per project sound
platforms: both

**Settings > Media > Sound** sets what the Mix step puts under the voice in every video.

- **Music** (on by default): music and ambience beds. MS Paint stick figures never have music.
- **Sound effects** (on by default): sounds on cuts, cards and accents.

Switch one off and new mixes leave it out.

## One project only

A project can choose its own in its Project settings: the gear next to its name in the editor.

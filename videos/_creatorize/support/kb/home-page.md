# The Home page
keywords: home, home page, welcome back, start page, dashboard, getting started list, checklist, setup steps, continue editing, continue where you left off, recent projects, recent videos, coming up, due this week, late videos, quick start, open the editor, import, new project, new video, first page, main page
platforms: both

**Home** is the first page of each workspace: what to do next, where you left off, and your Claude usage.

## Faceless Brand Home

- **Import** and **New project** at the top right.
- **Coming up**: videos due on your Channels boards in the next 7 days, and late ones. Shown only when something is due. **Open the board** opens Channels.
- **Getting started**: six setup steps with a progress bar (Connect Claude, Check your tools, Set up transcription, Create or import a project, Make your first edit, Export a video). It disappears once all six are done.
- Four shortcuts: **Continue editing** (your last project; **Open the editor** when you have none), **New project**, **Styles**, **Memory**.
- **Continue where you left off**: your three most recent projects. **All projects** opens the Projects page.
- **Your Claude usage**: tokens, models and savings (see "Claude usage on Home: tokens, models and charts" and "Your Claude plan and savings on Home").

## Personal Brand Home

- **New video** at the top right.
- **Connect Claude** with **Sign in**, while Claude is not connected.
- **Coming up**, as above.
- Shortcuts: **Continue editing**, **New video**, **Thumbnails**, **Layouts**.
- **Recent videos**: your last six videos. **All videos** opens the Videos page.
- **Your Claude usage**, the same numbers as in Faceless Brand.

## Getting started steps that stay unticked

- **Connect Claude**: click **Sign in** (see "Claude not installed or not signed in").
- **Check your tools**: it names what is missing. **Review** opens Settings (see "System check and missing tools").
- **Set up transcription**: starts by itself once Claude is connected. If it failed, click **Try again** (see "Transcription setup (word timing)").
- **Make your first edit**: ticks after your first Claude edit.
- **Export a video**: ticks after your first finished export.

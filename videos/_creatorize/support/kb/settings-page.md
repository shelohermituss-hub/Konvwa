# Settings page: tabs and search
keywords: settings, open settings, where is settings, preferences, options, configure, gear, cog, find a setting, can't find setting, search settings, settings search, settings tabs, general, ai engines, media, youtube, system, experimental, settings claude, run checks, command palette, search or run, ctrl k, cmd k
platforms: both

Click **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar (under **New project** (**New video** in Personal Brand) and **Help**). Settings has six tabs; it reopens on the last one you used.

## The tabs

- **General**: Language, Updates, Interface scale, Preview quality, Graphics (each video), Workspace (log and diagnostics).
- **AI engines**: Claude, ChatGPT (Codex), Model, Effort, Your Claude plan, Preferred video length, Your scripting skill, Permissions, Sessions, Bridge for your own sessions.
- **Media**: Image generation (with Google Flow), Voiceover, Sound, Sound effects library, Character animations, Custom APIs.
- **YouTube**: your Google API key, Google sign-in app and the channels you follow.
- **System**: System check (with Transcription), Graphics, Tool paths.
- **Experimental**: Google subscription models via CLIProxyAPI (at your own risk).

Claude's settings are under **AI engines**; there is no "Claude" tab.

## Find a setting

- Type in **Search settings** at the top (press **/** to jump there). Typos are fine. Try words like voice, key, music, folder or update. Pick a result: its tab opens and the setting lights up.
- Or press Ctrl/⌘ + K (or click **Search or run…** in the sidebar) and type: settings show up as "Settings: …".
- Buttons in messages, like **Open Settings**, jump straight to the right setting.
- The Claude button at the bottom of the sidebar (**Claude connected** or **Claude not signed in**) opens **Settings > AI engines > Claude**.

## Good to know

- Changes save right away ("Saved.").
- **Run checks** (top right) checks all tools again: see "System check and missing tools".
- Some settings can be different per project: model, effort, image service, sound. Those are in the project's own settings in the editor.

# The timeline
keywords: timeline, scrub, scrubbing, drag playhead, playhead, select range, time range, in point, out point, zoom timeline, scroll timeline, move along timeline, timeline too small, fit, shots row, narration row, phrases, words, cues, word cues, red cue, miss, waveform, no audio yet, cards, auto beds, audio layers, purple marks, edit markers, add audio layer, right click timeline
platforms: both

The timeline under the preview shows the whole video. Click to select and move the playhead, drag to scrub.

## Rows

- **Shots**: one block per shot. Click to select it; Shift or Ctrl/⌘ + click adds more.
- **Narration**: the spoken phrases. Click one to select it.
- **Cues**: the words that make something happen on screen. Click one to jump there. A red cue's word was not found in the narration.
- **Audio**: the voiceover waveform ("No audio yet" before there is one).
- Your own audio layers, one row each once you add some. Drag a clip to move it: it snaps to words, shots and cues.
- **Cards** and **Auto beds**: cards and accents, and the automatic sound beds.

Purple notches on the ruler mark where Claude made an edit (grey once reverted). Hover one to see what it was.

## Moving around

- Click or drag to move the playhead. Dragging plays bits of the voice, so you can find a word by ear.
- Zoom: Ctrl + wheel on Windows, ⌘ + scroll or pinch on a Mac, or the zoom out / zoom in buttons. The **Fit** button shows the whole video again.
- Scroll, or drag the bar under the timeline, to move along. Dragging with the middle mouse button works too.
- While playing, the timeline follows the playhead.

## Selecting a time range

- Shift + drag on the **Audio** row or the ruler, or press I and O at the playhead for in and out points (they snap to a nearby shot edge). X clears the range.
- Claude then works on that part. The loop button repeats it.

## Add a sound at a time

Right-click the timeline and pick **Add audio layer here** (it shows the time) to place music or an effect there (see "Music, sound effects and the mix").

## Finding a moment

- Ctrl/⌘ + F: **Find in narration**. Type a word, pick a line: it jumps there and selects that phrase.
- G: **Go to time**, in seconds or m:ss (for example 83.5 or 1:23.5).

# Making a new project
keywords: new project, new video, create video, create project, start video, make a video, topic, script, paste script, i have a script, claude writes it, length, format, 9:16, 16:9, vertical, style, look, language, voiceover, audio file, direction for claude, brand colour, channel, main character, create project button
platforms: both

Click **New project** in the sidebar (Ctrl/⌘ + N). The window has four parts.

## 01 The video

- **Name your video** (the title).
- **Channel**: one of your channels or **No channel**. Style, look and length follow your last project there.
- **Voiceover** (optional, add it later if you like): **Choose audio file…** or drop one (WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG).
  Scenes are timed to the voiceover, so they are built once it is in.

## 02 Look

- **Style**: the kind of video (see "Video styles").
- **Format**: Vertical 9:16 or Wide 16:9, for styles that make both (see "Wide 16:9 or vertical 9:16").
- **Look**: the style's finish. Hidden for a 9:16 / 16:9 style that has only one look.
- **Length**: pick one (Shorts, Videos, Long-form up to 45:00) or type any length, e.g. 7:30. Your default length is
  Settings > AI engines > **Preferred video length**.
- **Pictures per minute** (Vox only) and **Brand colour**: both changeable later in the Look.
- **Language**: only with Settings > General > Language set to **Several languages** (see "Making a video in another
  language").
- **Characters** (3D reconstruction) or **Main character** (Flat explainer): see "3D reconstruction characters" and
  "Characters".

## 03 Story (optional)

- **Claude writes it**: type a topic; Claude researches it and writes the narration to your length.
- **I have a script**: paste your exact narration. It becomes the transcript, so timing matches word for word. A
  warning shows if it is much longer or shorter than your length.
- **Direction for Claude**: tone, characters, what to emphasise, camera style.

## 04 This project's settings

Claude model and effort, image generation, voiceover, real media and sound for this video only, plus
**Your scripting skill**: add one here (**Add a file**, **Add a folder**, **Paste text**; it is kept in Settings >
AI engines) or switch off **Use my scripting skill for this video**. Change the rest later in the editor's
**Project settings**.

Click **Create project** (or Ctrl/⌘ + Enter). With a topic, Claude starts researching and writing the script right away.

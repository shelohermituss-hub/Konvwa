## A captions file for YouTube

1. In **Export > Video**, keep **Save captions (.srt) next to the video** ticked (it is on by default).
2. Export. The result line says "captions saved".
3. The .srt file is next to the MP4 in the project's out folder, with the same name (with **Also make a 4K version**, it's named after the 4K file). Click **Show in folder** to find it.
4. Upload it to YouTube as the video's subtitles, or import it into your video editor.

The captions come from the timed words (**Words timed**). They are saved only for full-length exports, not for **Only the selected range**.

## Captions in a vertical video

**Export > Vertical 9:16** > **Captions** puts big word-by-word captions into the lower part of the picture (or keeps the video's own captions if its look has them). They need timed words: "No timed words yet: run Time words first."

## Captions inside the 16:9 video

Captions drawn as part of the picture belong to the style and its look. See "Look settings (grain, colours, captions, camera)".

## Out of sync?

The captions file is made when the words are timed, and **Trim silences** moves it with the cuts. After a voiceover change that wasn't timed again, click **Words timed > Redo**, then export again.

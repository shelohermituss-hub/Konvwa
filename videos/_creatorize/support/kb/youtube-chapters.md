# YouTube chapters
keywords: youtube chapters, chapters, chapter markers, timestamps, time stamps, description timestamps, chapter list, video chapters, chapter titles, copy chapters, key moments, sections, segments, 0:00, intro, chapters not showing, chapters not working, youtube description
platforms: both

The export window makes a chapter list for your YouTube description.

1. Open **Export** (the **Video** tab) and click **YouTube chapters** at the bottom left. It's there when the video has shots and no export is running.
2. A list appears: one line per chapter, a time and a title, starting at 0:00 ("Intro" when nothing starts right at the beginning).
3. Edit the titles in the box, then click **Copy**.
4. Paste the list into the video's description on YouTube.

## How the list is made

- From the video's chapter titles, or, when it has fewer than two, from its scenes.
- A chapter less than 10 s after the one before is left out.
- YouTube shows chapters only with at least 3 of them, each 10 s or longer. The window warns when there are fewer than 3.

Click **YouTube chapters** again to hide the list. You can open it before or after exporting.

# Channels: make, edit and link a channel
keywords: new channel, make channel, add channel, create channel, edit channel, rename channel, delete channel, channel picture, channel avatar, channel colour, channel color, youtube handle, about, reorder channels, move channel up, channel menu, link youtube channel, connect channel to youtube, its youtube numbers, not linked, make a channel from, linked
platforms: both

Make one channel per YouTube channel you run. Each gets its own board on the **Channels** page.

## Make a channel
1. Open **Channels** and click **New channel** (or **Make your first channel**).
2. Fill in:
   - The picture: click the circle or drop a PNG, JPG or WebP image on it.
   - **Name** (needed).
   - **YouTube handle** (optional), e.g. @spacefactsdaily.
   - **Colour**: marks its videos on the boards.
   - **About** (optional, for you).
3. Click **Make channel** (Ctrl/⌘ + Enter also saves).

## The channel menu
Right-click a channel in the left list, or click its **…** button:
- **Show its board**, **Edit channel…**
- **New project in this channel** (Faceless Brand) / **New video in this channel** (Personal Brand)
- **Its YouTube numbers** (only when the channel is linked to a YouTube channel)
- **Move up** / **Move down**. You can also drag channels in the list, or press Alt/⌥ + ↑ / ↓.

## Delete a channel
**Edit channel > Delete channel**, then **Delete channel** again (or **Keep it**). Its videos and ideas stay, with no channel. Lessons kept from its YouTube numbers are deleted with it.

## Link it to its YouTube channel
The YouTube handle field does not link anything. Linking happens on the **YouTube** page (Premium):
1. Follow the YouTube channel or sign in with Google for it (see "YouTube page: connect your channels").
2. At **Add it to which of your Creatorize channels?** pick your channel, or **Make a channel from "…"** (uses its name, handle and picture).
3. Later you can change it in the **What's working** card on the YouTube page, or in **Settings > YouTube**.

Once linked, its projects show their views on the Channels board, and lessons from its numbers can guide the scripts of its Faceless videos. A channel already linked to another YouTube channel shows **Linked** and cannot be picked.

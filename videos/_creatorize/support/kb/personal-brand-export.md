# Personal Brand: sign off and render
keywords: sign off, sign off & render, render, render again, export personal, final render, export video, youtube 1080p, 1440p, 4k, hevc, h.264, small file, render preset, where is my video, render folder, renders page, gpu encoder, cpu slow render, cancel render, your video is ready, not yet
platforms: both

When the cut looks right, **Sign off & render** (top right of the Personal editor) makes the final video file.

## Render
1. Watch the video through first.
2. Click **Sign off & render**. The window shows how many clips and how long the cut is.
3. Pick a preset:
   - **YouTube 1080p (H.264)**
   - **YouTube 1440p (H.264, sharper on YouTube)**
   - **4K (HEVC)**
   - **Small file 1080p (HEVC)**
4. Click **Sign off & render**. **Not yet** closes the window; **Cancel** stops a render that is running.

The progress shows how much is done and how many times faster than realtime it renders.

## When it is done
**Your video is ready** shows the file name, length, size and render time:
- **Show in folder** opens it in File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac).
- **Play** opens it in your video player.
- **Done** closes the window.

The file is saved in the video's out folder. Every render is also listed on the **Renders** page (Personal Brand sidebar > Studio), newest first.

## Good to know
- **Sign off & render** is greyed out until the timeline has clips (add footage, or run **Cut the footage**).
- The final render uses your original footage at full quality, not the preview copies.
- It encodes on the graphics card when one is found. "No GPU encoder found: encodes on the CPU (slower)" means it still works, only slower.
- After a render the button says **Render again**; hover it to see when you signed off.
- Changing the cut after signing off is fine: render again.

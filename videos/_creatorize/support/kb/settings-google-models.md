# Google models via CLIProxyAPI (experimental)
keywords: gemini, gemini 4, gemini 4 argon, argon, gemini flash, google models, google ai, google ai pro, google ai ultra, ultra, antigravity, cliproxy, cliproxyapi, proxy, experimental, other models, use gemini, google account blocked, banned, requests a day, limit, switch account, turn off, remove
platforms: both

**Settings > Experimental > Google subscription models via CLIProxyAPI** runs edits, questions, scripts and builds on Google's models with your Google AI plan instead of Claude. Your own Claude Code does the work through a small program on this computer. Ultraedit, the Style Studio and character design stay on Claude.

## Read this first: use at your own risk

- **It can get your Google account blocked.** Google has blocked paid accounts for this before, without refunds.
- Use a **separate Google account**, not the one with your YouTube channel or Gmail.
- **One Google account only.** Never sign in several to stretch the quota.
- It can stop working any day. Creatorize does not support it and can't help if Google blocks you.
- Offered on Windows (64-bit) and macOS only.

## Models and limits

- **Gemini 4 Argon** needs Google AI Ultra. On AI Pro you get **Gemini 3.8 Flash**, plus Claude Sonnet/Opus 5.5 on Google's quota.
- Google reports about 200 requests a day on Ultra. A full build can use 100 or more, so expect to hit the limit.
- **Effort** doesn't apply to Google models.

## Turn it on

1. Tick **I understand the risks and use a separate Google account**, then click **Turn on**. It downloads the program and starts it.
2. When it says **Running**, click **Sign in with Google** and finish in your browser.
3. Pick a model in **Settings > AI engines > Model** (group "Experimental (Google)") or in a project's Claude panel.

## Manage it

- **Switch account**: signs this account out and opens Google's sign-in for another.
- **Try again**: when it says "Stopped" or "Stopped with a problem".
- **Turn off**: stops it; the program and your sign-in stay for next time.
- **Remove**: deletes the program and the Google sign-in from this computer.

Stopped working? Switch back to a Claude model in **Settings > AI engines > Model**.

# Renders page: your finished videos
keywords: renders, renders page, exports, my exports, finished video, where is my video, where is my mp4, find export, exported file, output folder, out folder, play video, show in folder, render list, render failed, failed export, no exports yet, no renders yet, export history, 9:16, trailer, clip, missing play button, delete export
platforms: both

The **Renders** page lists every export, newest first. Each workspace lists its own: Faceless Brand exports in Faceless Brand, Personal Brand renders in Personal Brand.

## A row shows

- The file name, the project's name, how long ago, and how many seconds it took.
- A percentage while it is still rendering (the list updates by itself).
- **Exported** when it finished, **Failed** when it did not, **Stopped** when you cancelled it.
- A blue **9:16**, **Trailer 9:16** or **Clip 9:16** badge for versions made in Export > Vertical 9:16.

## Watch or find the file

- **Play**: opens the video in your computer's video player.
- **Show in folder** (the folder icon): shows the file in File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac).

Exports are saved in the out folder inside the project's folder.

## No Play button?

The file is not where it was saved: it was moved, renamed or deleted. Look in the project's out folder (Projects > **Show folder** on the card), or in the Recycle Bin (Windows) or Trash (Mac).

## Empty page

- Faceless Brand: "No exports yet". Open a project and press **Export**.
- Personal Brand: "No renders yet". Open a video, watch it through and press **Sign off & render**.

## Good to know

- Exports keep running while you work on other things. The sidebar shows **Exporting** with a percentage; click it to see the export.
- The list shows your last 100 exports. A project you delete takes its exports off the list.
- The list can't delete exports. To free space, delete the video files in the project's out folder yourself.
- A failed export: see "Export failed or stuck".

# Pixel art look settings
keywords: pixel art, pixel, pixels, retro, 8-bit, art resolution, resolution, chunky, too pixelated, blurry pixels, crisp, outlines, outline, black lines, outline colour, rim light, shadows, cast shadows, shadow detail, vignette, dark corners, dither, edge dither, transition, pixelate transition, field of view, fov, zoom, camera push-in, brand colour, crisp 640
platforms: both

Change these in the editor: **Look** button > change the setting > **Apply to project**. Pixel art is 16:9 only.
New videos start on the look **Crisp 640** (defaults below). Older videos may still use the older looks Hi-detail 480,
Classic 320 or Flat diagram.

- **Art resolution**: how many pixels the 3D picture is drawn with. **320×180 chunky**, **384×216**,
  **480×270 hi-detail**, **640×360 crisp** (default). Too chunky? Pick a higher one. Higher is heavier on the computer.
- **Depth outlines** (on): dark contour where depth jumps. Turn off for no outlines around shapes.
- **Object outlines** (on): separates touching objects at similar depth.
- **Rim light** (on): a one-shade-lighter edge on sides facing the light.
- **Fade outlines with fog** (on): distant outlines dissolve for depth.
- **Outline colour** (dark navy): the colour of all outlines.
- **Cast shadows** (on): objects cast shadows. Turn off for a flatter, calmer picture.
- **Shadow detail**: 1024, 2048 or 4096 (default): how sharp the shadows are.
- **Vignette** (0 to 0.6, default 0.30): darker edges. Set to 0 for none.
- **Edge dither** (0 to 0.1, default 0.04): a fine dot pattern in the darkened edges, so the vignette fades smoothly.
  Set to 0 to remove it.
- **Transition length** (0.20s to 1.00s, default 0.42s): how long the change between shots takes.
- **Pixelate transition** (4 to 24, default 10): how blocky the pixelate effect between shots gets.
- **Field of view** (28° to 60°, default 40°): lower = more zoomed in and flatter, higher = wider.
- **Camera push-in** (0% to 10%, default 4%): the slow zoom on every shot. Set to 0% for a still camera.
- **Brand colour** (Style default): your channel colour for the chapter cards, chapter tag and stat units. The 3D art
  stays on its 16-colour palette (the nearest palette colour is used).

There is no film grain setting in Pixel art's look.

## Make it your default

On the **Styles** page, **Duplicate** a look, change it, **Save look**, then pick it in New project. See "Styles page".

# Claude usage on Home: tokens, models and charts

platforms: both

At the bottom of **Home**, **Your Claude usage** shows what Claude did in your videos, and what the same tokens would cost on the pay-per-token API. Both workspaces show the same numbers.

## The numbers

- **Tokens used**: all tokens, the number of runs, and how much came from cache.
- **API equivalent**: an estimate at the API's list prices. It is not a bill: on your plan you pay the plan price.
- **You saved** (or **Plan pays for itself at**): needs your Claude plan. See "Your Claude plan and savings on Home".
- **Favourite model**: the model that used the most tokens, with its share.

Pick the time span with **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days** (the default) or **All time**.

## The charts

- **Tokens per day** (per month for a long **All time**). **$** switches it to API-equivalent dollars.
- **Plan vs API**: your plan's price against the API equivalent.
- **Models**: each model's share of the tokens.
- **Top projects**: the five projects that used the most tokens. Click one to open it. A deleted one shows as "Removed project".

## What counts

Every Claude run in the app: edits and builds, Personal Brand cuts, Routines, and Library harvests (Claude looking back over a built video). Runs on Google's models (Settings > Experimental) don't use your Claude plan and are left out.

Very old runs only kept totals: they show as "Model not recorded" and their dollars are estimates. The note under the charts says when that applies.

## "Nothing yet"

Your first Claude edit or build shows up here.

## Good to know

- To change which model Claude uses and how hard it thinks, see "Model and effort (which Claude, how hard it thinks)".
- This is your Claude plan's usage, not your Creatorize plan (Standard or Premium).

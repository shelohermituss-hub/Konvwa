# AI engines settings (Claude, ChatGPT and how they work)
keywords: ai engines, claude settings, settings claude, ai settings, claude options, model, effort, permissions, sessions, bridge, chatgpt, codex, video length, scripting skill, claude plan, usage, uses too much, slow edits, faster edits, settings > claude, where is claude setting
platforms: both

Everything about the AI that edits and builds your videos is in **Settings > AI engines** (there is no "Settings > Claude" tab). Rows from top to bottom:

- **Claude**: sign in or out with your own Claude Code. See "Claude not installed or not signed in".
- **ChatGPT (Codex)**: run edits and builds on your ChatGPT plan instead. Ultraedit, the Style Studio and character design stay on Claude. See "ChatGPT (Codex) as the AI engine".
- **Model**: Account default, Fable 5.1, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 or Haiku 5.5 for every project without its own. See "Model and effort".
- **Effort**: how much Claude thinks before acting, Low to Max (default Medium). See "Model and effort".
- **Your Claude plan**: Pro, Max 5x, Max 20x, Team or your own price, so Home shows what you save. See "Your Claude plan and savings on Home".
- **Preferred video length**: the length new projects start with (default 2:00). See "Preferred video length".
- **Your scripting skill** (custom skill, .md upload): your own way of writing scripts, followed every time Claude writes one. Upload a .md or .txt file, a skill folder, or paste text. See "Your scripting skill".
- **Permissions**: Automatic, Ask before commands or Ask for everything. See "Permissions: what Claude may do without asking".
- **Sessions**: checking edits with rendered frames, growing the Library, your own Claude Code setup, the turn limit. See "Sessions settings".
- **Bridge for your own sessions**: let a Claude Code session you already have open work on the open project. See "Using your own Claude Code session (Bridge)".

## Using less of your plan

- Pick **Sonnet 5.5** or **Haiku 5.5** under **Model**, or a lower **Effort**.
- A single project can use its own model and effort: New project (This project's settings), Project settings in the editor, or the menu under the project's Claude panel input.
- Home shows your usage: **Tokens used**, **API equivalent**, **Models** and **Top projects**.

Google models (Gemini) are under **Settings > Experimental**, at your own risk.

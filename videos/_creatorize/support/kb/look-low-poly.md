# Low poly shorts look settings
keywords: low poly, low-poly, game style, bright, moody, render resolution, too pixelated, chunky, blurry, sharpening, halos, colour fringes, chromatic aberration, rainbow edges, glow, bloom, vignette, dark corners, captions, caption size, captions too big, caption height, captions position, camera shake, shaky, wobble, camera push-in, wipe length, ps1, playstation, vertex wobble, texture warp, dither
platforms: both

Change these in the editor: **Look** button > change the setting > **Apply to project**. Two looks: **Bright**
(default) and **Moody**. The characters are in "Low poly shorts characters".

- **Format**: Vertical 9:16 or Wide 16:9. Picked in New project, fixed afterwards.
- **Lighting and finish**: **Bright** (flat daylight, little post) or **Moody** (dark rooms, warm lamps, glow).
- **Render resolution**: **240 (very chunky)**, **270 (Moody channel)**, **320 (Bright channel)**, **360**,
  **540 (sharp)**. Too blocky? Pick a higher one. Bright 320, Moody 270.
- **Captions** (on): two to five words at a time. Turn off for none.
- **Caption size** (0.7× to 1.6×, default 1.0×).
- **Caption height** (55% to 85% from the top; Bright 68%, Moody 75%): where captions sit.
- **Sharpening** (0 to 1.2; Bright 0.28, Moody 0.75): edge halos. Lower it if edges look harsh.
- **Colour fringes** (0 to 2; Bright 0.35, Moody 1.00): red/blue edges toward the corners. Set to 0 to remove.
- **Glow** (0 to 1; Bright 0.18, Moody 0.40): lamps, screens and neon glow.
- **Vignette** (0 to 0.8; Bright 0, Moody 0.50): darker corners.
- **Camera push-in** (0% to 12%; Bright 6%, Moody 5%): how fast every shot creeps in.
- **Camera shake** (0 to 1, default 0): a handheld wobble. Shaky? Set it to 0.
- **Wipe length** (0.20s to 0.70s, default 0.36s): most cuts are hard; this times the wipes and mosaic dissolves.
- **PS1 strict: vertex wobble**, **PS1 strict: texture warp**, **PS1 strict: dither** (all off): PlayStation 1
  effects. Turn them off if shapes wobble, textures swim or colours look dotted.
- **Brand colour** (Style default): your channel colour for the 8-bit number banners, the brand sting and the game
  panel frames. Green gains and red losses keep their colours.

There is no film grain setting in this style.

# Styles page: looks, your styles, saving a look
keywords: styles page, styles, looks, look, presets, preset, save look, new look, duplicate look, edit look, delete look, my looks, default look, read-only, built-in, custom, custom styles, your styles, delete style, characters tab, look name, can't edit look, greyed out, custom tab, can't be a studio base, studio base, 3d cartoon custom look
platforms: both

Open **Styles** in the sidebar (under Workspace). It shows every style, its looks and their settings.

## What is on the page

- **Built-in** and **Custom** tabs at the top. Custom holds your own styles (made with the Style Kit and imported).
- A card per style (hover it to play a sample). Each card has a **New look** button.
- **Looks in <style>**: the style's looks. Click one to see its settings on the right, with how many projects use it.
- Flat explainer has a second tab, **Characters** (see "Characters").

## Make your own look (every plan)

1. Click a look, then **Duplicate**. You get "<name> copy" ("Duplicated. Edit your copy.").
2. Rename it, change the description and settings.
3. Click **Save look**.
4. Use it: pick it in **New project**, or in a video's **Look** button > **Apply to project**.

Built-in looks show **Built-in · read-only**: duplicate them instead. Saving a look doesn't change videos that already
use it: apply it again in each video's Look.

## Delete a look

Click your look > **Delete**. Built-in looks can't be deleted. A look made in the Style Studio (**Made in the Style
Studio · read-only**) asks first. Videos using a deleted look keep their settings.

## New look with Claude (Premium)

**New look** (on a style card) opens the Style Studio: describe the look, add reference pictures or videos, Claude
sets it up and the app checks it. See "Style Studio".

3D cartoon shorts, 3D Informational, Stick stories and Roblox story shorts have no New look yet ("can't be a Studio
base"): **Duplicate** a look and change its settings instead. 3D cartoon characters: New project > **Characters** >
**Describe one**.

## Your own styles (Custom tab, Premium)

- Two cards on top: **Import style** and **Get the Style Kit**. Whole styles are built outside the app, in Claude Code
  with the Style Kit, then imported here; with no style yet the tab lists the three steps. See "Custom styles and the Style Kit".
- **Unfinished imports** lists imports still being checked or waiting for fixes: **Open** or **Discard**.
- Under **Your styles**, each style has a card with its version and the style it was built on (for example "v1 · built on Flat engine ..."),
  **New look**, **Re-check**, **Export package** and the **…** menu (**Go back to v…**, **Show in Explorer** /
  **Show in Finder**, **Delete style**).
- Your styles appear in New project under **Custom styles**.

Format (9:16 or 16:9) is not part of a look: it is picked per video in New project.

# The preview: play, click things, zoom
keywords: preview, play, pause, playback, click to select, select object, select character, point at, pick, highlight, outline, shift click, multiple selection, zoom preview, magnify, zoom in, pan, full screen, fullscreen, playback speed, slow motion, speed up, 2x, loop, repeat, mute, no sound, sound off, save frame, screenshot, still, png, copy image, timecode, next shot, previous shot, preview quality
platforms: both

The preview in the middle of the editor plays your video live. It is also how you show Claude what you mean.

## Play

- **Space** or the play button: play / pause. Clicking the picture while it plays pauses it.
- Previous / next shot buttons, or [ and ].
- Playback speed at the right: **0.5×**, **1×**, **1.5×**, **2×**.
- Loop button: select a shot, phrase or time range first, then it repeats that part.
- Mute button, or M.
- F: full-screen preview. Press F again to leave.

## Point Claude at something

1. Pause.
2. Move the mouse over the picture: a box shows what you would pick.
3. Click a character, an object, a text or the scenery. Shift + click (or Ctrl/⌘ + click) adds more.
4. Your picks show as chips above the Claude box: remove one with its x, or **Clear** them all (Esc also clears).
5. Type the change and send. Claude also gets the frame you were looking at, with your picks outlined.

## Zoom in on small things

- = and - zoom in and out, 0 fits again. Ctrl + wheel zooms at the cursor (Mac: ⌘ + scroll).
- While zoomed: scroll to move around, or drag with the middle mouse button, or Alt/⌥ + drag.
- The "% · Fit" badge in the corner goes back to fit.

## Save a still

The camera button saves the current frame as a 1080p PNG in the project's out folder. The message that appears has **Copy image**, to paste it anywhere.

## Preview quality

The gauge menu next to the speed: **Full**, **Half**, **Quarter** or **Auto** (full, and smaller while playing if frames come too slowly). Lower plays smoother on a slow computer. Exports, saved frames and Claude's frames always stay full size. Also in **Settings > General > Preview quality**.

Black, stuck or slow preview? See "Preview black, stuck, broken or slow".

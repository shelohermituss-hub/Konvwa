# Your Claude plan and savings on Home
keywords: your claude plan, claude plan, pro, max, max 5x, max 20x, team, plan price, other price, savings, you saved, how much did i save, api equivalent, cost, money, tokens used, usage, billing, subscription, hide savings, not sure hide savings, wrong savings, plan pays for itself, pick your plan to see it, home stats
platforms: both

Home shows how much Claude you used (**Tokens used**, **API equivalent**, **Models**, **Top projects**) and what you save compared with the pay-per-token Claude API. The app can't read your plan reliably, so you pick it.

## Pick your plan

In **Settings > AI engines > Your Claude plan** (or the plan pill on Home's savings card):

- **Pro** $20, **Max 5x** $100, **Max 20x** $200 or **Team** $30 (a month).
- **Other $**: type your monthly price, then click **Save**.
- **Not sure · hide savings**: leaves out only the savings card; tokens and the API equivalent stay.

"Now:" under the title shows what is set, like "Max 5x · $100/mo". If Claude Code reports your plan, the text mentions it as a hint.

## Reading the numbers

- **API equivalent** is an estimate at Anthropic's API list prices, not a bill. On your plan you pay the plan price.
- **You saved** is the API equivalent minus your plan's price for that period.
- **Plan pays for itself at** shows instead while you have used less than your plan costs.
- "Pick your plan to see it": choose your plan as above.

This is your Claude plan, not your Creatorize plan (Standard or Premium).

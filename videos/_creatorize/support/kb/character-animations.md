# Character animations (3D reconstruction)
keywords: animations, animation, moves, motion, mocap, motion capture, mixamo, fbx, import fbx, import animation, add animation, walk, run, sit, dance, character animations, clips, my clips, in place, without skin, inbox folder, remove animation
platforms: both

3D reconstruction characters move with animation clips. Claude picks them by name or kind (sit, phone, walk…). Free
clips come with the app; you can add your own. Find it in **Settings** > **Media** > **Character animations**.

## Add clips from Mixamo

1. Go to mixamo.com and sign in with your own free Adobe account.
2. Pick a motion, then download it as **FBX**, **Without Skin**, **30** frames per second. Tick **In Place** for walks
   and runs.
3. In Creatorize click **Import FBX…** and pick the files (FBX, GLB or GLTF, up to 150 MB each).

Or drop the files into the inbox folder: they are added by themselves. **Show in File Explorer** (Windows) or
**Show in Finder** (Mac) opens the folder.

After an import the app says how many clips were added, already there, or could not be read.

## Your clips

- Each clip shows its length, whether it loops or travels, and its tags.
- With more than 8 clips a search box appears.
- **Remove** (click twice) deletes a clip from your library.
- Your clips stay on this computer.

Your own uploaded 3D characters play every clip the built-in mannequins have (see "3D reconstruction characters").

The editor is where you watch a video and ask Claude to change it. Open a project from **Projects**, or click **Editor** in the sidebar.

## Layout

- **Left**: the **Pipeline** steps, the **Trim silences**, **Music & effects** and **Ultraedit** buttons (each appears once it can be used), then the **Shots** list. Click a shot to jump there and select it. Ctrl/⌘ + B hides or shows this side.
- **Middle**: the preview, the play controls and the **Timeline**.
- **Right**: the **Claude** panel. Ctrl/⌘ + J hides or shows it.

## Top bar, left to right

- The video's title: click it to rename, press Enter to keep it.
- **Project settings** (gear icon): this video's own choices (see "Project settings").
- "Saved locally · git history": every change is saved on your computer.
- Your channel and due date, once you use **Channels**.
- **Voiceover** (waveform icon).
- **Update engine**, only on some older videos (see "Update engine on an older video").
- The video's language, when you use several languages.
- The style and look button: it shows the style and look names and opens the **Look** window.
- **Graphics** (see "Graphics panel").
- **Project notes**: tabs **Storyboard**, **Claude context**, **Style rules**, **Script**, **Research**, **Brief**, **Timed script**.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** (or press ?).
- **Open project folder**: opens it in File Explorer on Windows, in Finder on a Mac.
- **Export**.

## Changing something

1. Pause and click a character, a text or the scenery in the preview (Shift + click adds more), or select a shot, phrase or time range on the timeline.
2. Type what to change in the Claude box and press Enter.
3. Claude edits the video, checks it and shows Before / After. **Revert** puts it back exactly.

The first time you open the editor, three tips show the way (**Next**, **Skip**, **Got it**).

# Update engine on an older video
keywords: update engine, engine update, upgrade engine, old project, older video, old video, button in top bar, is out, new version of my style, graphics switches missing, engine is older than the graphics switches, preview quality always full, accents, accented words, cues miss words
platforms: both

Videos made with an older version of the app can show **Update engine** in the editor's top bar, with the version change (for example "Update engine v… → v…"). It brings that one video up to the newest version of its style, with the newer features.

## What it does

- Your scenes and settings stay. The old version is kept in the project folder.
- The preview reloads. Play the video through once to check it.
- The video gets what newer versions added, for example the full **Graphics** switches (3D reconstruction) or the smaller **Preview quality** sizes.

## Where you see it

- The top bar of the editor.
- The **Graphics** panel: "This video's engine is older than the Graphics switches." with **Update engine**.
- After changing a video's language: a message may offer **Update engine** so cues find words with accents.
- Your own style, imported again as a newer version: the button reads "(your style's name) v… is available · Update". It is one step in the video's History, so you can undo it (see "Custom styles and the Style Kit").

Videos that are up to date don't show the button.

## Something looks different afterwards?

Ask Claude in the Claude panel to fix what changed, for example "After the engine update, shot 4 looks different: make it look like before." If that doesn't help, click **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Report a problem**, so it gets fixed.

# Channels: stages, Auto and By hand

platforms: both

Every card on the **Channels** board sits in one of eight stages. Most cards move by themselves as you work.

## The stages
- **Idea**: ideas you have not started yet.
- **Script**: new Faceless projects wait here until their script is written.
- **Voiceover**: the script is written, the voiceover comes next.
- **Building**: the voiceover (or, for Personal videos, the footage) is in and the video is being built.
- **Review**: scenes are built: watch it, fix what is off, export.
- **Ready**: exported or rendered, ready to upload.
- **Scheduled**: scheduled on YouTube, with its date.
- **Published**: live on YouTube, with its views.

## Auto and By hand
- A card marked **Auto** follows the project's files.
- Drag a card (or use **Move to stage**) and it is marked **By hand**. Hover the badge to see why it stays.
- A card placed ahead of its files goes back to Auto once its files reach that stage. One placed behind them stays until the files move on.
- To undo it now: the card's menu > **Back to automatic**.

## Scheduled and Published
A project's files never reach these two stages on their own: only its YouTube video can.
1. Follow your YouTube channel first (see "YouTube page: connect your channels").
2. On the card's menu choose **YouTube video…**.
3. Pick the video (the **Likely** one comes first; type to search). A video already linked to another project moves to this one.

The card then moves to Scheduled or Published by itself and shows the views. **Unlink** in the same window removes the link.

The YouTube page can also suggest the link: in its **Videos** table, click the project name with the **?** next to a video.

## "No YouTube videos yet"
The window has nothing to pick from: follow your YouTube channel on the **YouTube** page (or in **Settings > YouTube**) first.

A private video keeps its project where its files put it until the video is public or scheduled.

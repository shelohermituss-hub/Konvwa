# Exporting a video
keywords: export, exporting, render, download video, save video, mp4, finished video, make the video, where is my video, where are my exports, out folder, export button, can't export, cant export, export greyed out, export disabled, renders page, show in folder, export in background, exporting chip, ctrl e, cmd e, file name, overwrite, save frame, screenshot, png still
platforms: both

Export turns the project into a finished MP4 with its sound.

1. Click **Export** at the top right of the editor (or press Ctrl/⌘ + E). The **Export video** window opens on the **Video** tab. **Video exported > Export** in the Pipeline opens it too.
2. Check the **File name**.
3. Pick the options you want (see "Export settings (the Video tab)").
4. Click **Export**.

You can close the window: the export keeps going. The sidebar shows **Exporting** with a percentage (click it to see the progress), and the app tells you when it's done.

## When it's done

- **Play** opens the video. **Show in folder** opens its folder (File Explorer on Windows, Finder on Mac).
- Files are saved in the project's **out** folder. A new export never replaces an older one: it gets _2, _3 ... at the end of the name.
- The **Renders** page in the sidebar lists every export, newest first, with **Play** and a folder button.
- The **Video exported** step gets its tick.

## Export is greyed out

Hover over it to see why:
- "Build the video first: there are no scenes to export yet".
- "Wait for the voiceover to finish: the video would still have the old narration".
- "Clean the audio and time the words first" or "Time the words first": click **Clean and time** or **Time words** in the bar under the preview.

## Just one picture?

The camera button under the preview saves the current frame as a 1080p PNG in the out folder.

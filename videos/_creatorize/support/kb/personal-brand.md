# Personal Brand (editing your own footage)
keywords: personal brand, talking head, facecam, face cam, screen recording, my footage, own footage, tutorial video, edit my video, cut footage, remove restarts, bloopers, intro, new video, plan video, personal editor, layouts, sign off, render personal, workspace switch, faceless vs personal, how to start personal
platforms: both

In the **Personal Brand** workspace Claude edits footage you recorded yourself: a screen recording with your mic, facecam clips and an intro. Switch workspace at the top of the sidebar.

Sidebar: **Home**, **Videos**, **Channels**, **Editor**; under Studio **Thumbnails**, **Layouts**, **Renders**; under Analytics **YouTube**; at the bottom **New video**, **Help**, **Settings**.

## Make a video, step by step
1. **New video**: **Title**, **Channel**, **Thumbnail idea**, **Core concept** (optional), **Thumbnail image** (optional). Click **Plan video**.
2. The editor opens. Drop your footage anywhere on it, or **Add** in the **Footage** tab. Check each file's role: **Screen recording**, **Facecam**, **Intro**, **Other** (see "Personal Brand: footage").
3. **Layout** tab: how the screen and your face are arranged (see "Layouts").
4. **Workflows** tab: open **Cut the footage**, then **Start**. If you recorded a separate intro, run **Edit the intro** too (see "Personal Brand: Claude workflows").
5. Watch it on the **Timeline**. Fix small things yourself (S splits, Q / W delete to the playhead) or ask Claude in the **Claude** tab (see "Personal Brand: timeline and cuts").
6. **Sign off & render** (top right) makes the final file (see "Personal Brand: sign off and render").
7. The thumbnail: right panel > **Plan** > **Add a thumbnail**, or **Make one in Thumbnails →**.

## The editor at a glance
- Left: **Footage**, **Layout**, **Workflows**.
- Middle: the preview, play controls and the timeline.
- Right: **Claude** (the conversation) and **Plan** (thumbnail, thumbnail idea, core concept).
- Top: the title (click to rename), **Saved** / **Saving**, the channel, GPU or CPU badge, keyboard shortcuts (?), the video's folder, **Sign off & render**.

Your original files are never changed or moved: the app reads them where they are.

"Not found: Claude cannot render edited intros" (Settings > System): install Node.js (see "System check and missing tools").

# Project settings (one video's own choices)
keywords: project settings, gear icon, this project only, per project, per video, override, global setting, project model, project effort, image generation for this project, default image model, voiceover for this project, real photos, real footage, archive film, real maps, free 3d models, main character, no main character, music on off, sound effects on off, own music, different settings per video
platforms: both

Project settings set what one video does differently from **Settings**. In the editor, click the gear icon next to the video's title.

Every choice starts on **Global setting (…)**: it follows Settings and shows what that is now. Anything you pick is for this video only and is saved as you pick.

## Claude

- **Model**: for the research, the script, the build and your edits. Ultraedit always runs on Opus 5.5.
- **Effort**: how much Claude thinks before it acts. Higher is more careful and uses more of your plan.

The same two menus sit under the Claude panel's box.

## Pictures and voice

- **Image generation**: **Automatic**, **Higgsfield**, **Fal.ai**, plus **Google Flow** or your Custom API once set up. A service without its key says "(no key yet)".
- **Default image model**: shown for Higgsfield or a Custom API.
- **Voiceover**: the voice the Voiceover window opens on: **Automatic**, **My recording**, **ElevenLabs**, **Fish Audio**, **Local voice**.

## Real media

**Real photos and footage**, **Free 3D models from the web** and **Real maps of the places** are for 3D reconstruction videos (MS Paint stick figures have **Use real images from the web**; Low poly shorts always use free 3D models). Other styles show them greyed out with the reason.

## Sound

**Music** and **Sound effects**: On or Off for this video. A change applies at the next Mix.

## Main character (Flat explainer)

Your character, or **No main character** (Claude casts each scene on its own). The next build follows.

## Your own music

**Add your own music...** puts your own track under the whole video. See "Music, sound effects and the mix".

The global choices are in **Settings > AI engines** (Claude) and **Settings > Media** (pictures, voice, sound).

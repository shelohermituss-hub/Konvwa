# 3D reconstruction characters
keywords: 3d characters, characters, mannequin, mannequins, figures, people, cast, roles, main character, suspect, victim, detective, witness, body, male, female, finish, matte, glossy, glow, colour, upload character, own character, my own character, fbx, glb, vrm, mixamo, rigged, bones, bone mapping, use this character, remove character
platforms: both

3D reconstruction people are faceless mannequins. Each role (Main character, Suspect, Victim, Detective, Witness,
Accomplice, Official, Prisoner, Police officer, Tactical officer, Unknown person, Extra) has a body, a finish and a
colour. Scenes name the role, so a change re-casts the whole video at once.

## Where

- New video: **New project** > **Characters** > **Choose characters…**
- Existing video: **Look** button > **Characters** > **Apply to project**. The cast stays when you switch looks.

## Change a role

1. Click the role's row: the live preview beside the table shows it. Pick a pose (Stand, Walk, Sit…) under it; the
   round-arrow button starts or stops it turning.
2. **Body**: **Male**, **Female**, or one of **Your own characters**.
3. **Finish**: **Matte** (clay-like), **Glossy** (porcelain-like), **Glow** (glowing body with a bright rim). Your own
   character also has **Own** (its uploaded materials).
4. **Colour**: click the swatch. With **Own** finish, pick a finish first to colour it.
5. The arrow button puts the role back to **Automatic** (its usual look).

## Upload your own character (every plan)

1. Under the table click **Your own characters**, then **Upload a character (FBX, GLB, VRM)**. GLTF works too, up
   to 200 MB. Optionally fill in **Licence / where it is from**.
2. It must be a rigged humanoid: Mixamo, Unreal, Unity / VRoid, Blender Rigify, Character Creator and Daz skeletons
   are matched by themselves.
3. The app draws a test sheet in its poses. "Every pose checked" means it is ready; otherwise it lists warnings.
4. Click the role's row, then **Use this character**.

**Some bones could not be matched**: open **Bones…**, click **Copy for Claude**, ask Claude, paste its answer, then
**Save the mapping and test**. Or pick each bone (* = needed) yourself.

Other buttons: **Test again**, **Remove** (click twice). A removed character is drawn as the male body.

Moves come from the animation library: see "Character animations".

# Sound effects library (your own sounds)
keywords: sound effects library, sfx library, my sounds, own sounds, my own sound effects, add sounds, add a folder, add files, import sounds, whoosh, whooshes, impacts, samples, foley, audio files, wav, mp3, tags, categories, rescan, search sounds, sounds not used, remove sound
platforms: both

**Settings > Media > Sound effects library** holds your own sounds for every video. The sound design uses them first, and in the editor you can place any of them yourself (**Music & effects > Layers**).

Any folder names work (Whooshes, Wooshes, Hits, Pops, UI...): the sound design matches them by kind. After **Sound design mixed**, the step says how many sound effects it added and how many came from your library, or why it added none. To put one exact sound on a moment, ask Claude, for example "add sound effects from my library": it sees your library directly and doesn't need access to your files.

## Add your sounds

- **Add a folder…**: pick a folder of sounds. Subfolders become categories ("Whooshes", "Impacts"), words in the file names become tags. A folder named "Whooshes" fills the whooshes.
- **Add files…**, or drop files onto the list.
- File types: wav, mp3, ogg, flac or m4a.

The app keeps its own copy in your workspace, so your folder can move. **Show in File Explorer** (Windows) / **Show in Finder** (Mac) opens that copy. Put sounds into it yourself? Click **Rescan**.

## Find and manage

- Search by name or tag (whoosh, door, paper …), pick a category, or show **All**, **Yours** or **Built-in**.
- Click play to listen.
- Your own sounds (marked **Yours**): the pencil edits name, category and tags; the bin removes it from your library (click **Remove?** to confirm).

Your sounds win over the built-in ones of the same kind when the sound design picks effects.

"No sounds found there (wav, mp3, ogg, flac, m4a)." means the folder had no sound files of those types.

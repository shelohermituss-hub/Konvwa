Templates are ready-made routines. They are listed under **Templates** in the left list of **Routines**. Click one to make a new routine from it, then change any node.

## The templates
- **Next video for my channel**: Claude studies your channel, finds a new topic, then script, voice, build, check, sound, render, thumbnail. Starts with a **Topic finder** (1 topic) and writes with **Deep research, fact-checked**.
- **Explainer from a topic**: **Topic**, then script, voice, build, check, sound, render, thumbnail. The **Start from a template** button makes this one.
- **Batch: topic list to videos** (bulk): one finished video per line of a **Topic list**, with **Script rules**, and each card moved to **Review**. Up to 3 videos are made at the same time: **Routine settings** (on the right when no node is selected) > **Videos at the same time** (1, 2 or 3; default 1, one after another).
- **Daily Short for my channel**: every day Claude finds a new topic and makes a 9:16 Flat explainer video (45 seconds), moved to **Ready**. It has a **Schedule** node.
- **My script to finished video**: **My script**, then voice, build, check, sound, render, thumbnail and **Title & description**.
- **Full channel autopilot**: on a **Schedule**, Claude finds ideas, makes the videos, a **Vertical version**, thumbnail and title, waits at **Approval**, uploads them privately with **Upload to YouTube** and moves the card to **Scheduled**.

Every template ends with **Notify**.

## Before the first run
A template is not ready by itself. Fill in what the **Before it can run** list asks for, for example:
- **Topic**: write the topic. **Topic list**: add your topics. **My script**: paste the script.
- **Topic finder**: pick a YouTube channel to study (followed on the YouTube page), or write what the channel is about.
- **Create project**: pick the channel, style and look you want.
- **Upload to YouTube**: pick the channel and allow uploads.

Then press **Run** (see "Routines: run, schedule, resume").

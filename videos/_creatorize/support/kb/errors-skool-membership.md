# Skool sign-in, membership and plan errors
keywords: skool, can't log in, cant log in, login, log in with skool, not a member, locked, membership, can't reach skool, no internet connection, antivirus, vpn, firewall, premium feature, upgrade, standard, premium, monthly exports, export limit, locked feature, sign-in window dark, small dark window, sign-in window can't show
platforms: both

## Can't reach Skool
> You're online, but Creatorize can't reach skool.com.

What it means: something on this computer blocks the app from reaching Skool. Usually an antivirus (Bitdefender, Norton, Avast), a firewall or a VPN.

Fix:
1. Allow Creatorize Suite in your antivirus or firewall, or turn the VPN off.
2. Click **Try again**.
3. Still stuck? Click **Copy details for support** and post it in the Skool community.

## The sign-in window stays dark
> Creatorize's sign-in window can't show

What it means: the small window that checks your Skool membership when Creatorize starts couldn't draw. Creatorize already put up new windows by itself; something on the computer keeps stopping it, most often an antivirus or security program.

Fix:
1. Restart the computer (Windows) or the Mac, then open Creatorize again.
2. Allow Creatorize Suite in your antivirus or security app (Windows: like Bitdefender, Norton, Avast or Windows Security; Mac: like Norton, Avast, Bitdefender or Intego), then open it again.
3. Still dark? Open the Creatorize Launcher (Windows: Start menu; Mac: Applications, or its icon in the menu bar), click **Copy diagnostics**, then **Report a problem** in the community and paste them when someone asks.

The sign-in window can be resized and maximised like any other window.

## No internet connection
> Creatorize checks your Skool membership when it starts, and Claude needs the internet to make videos.

Fix: connect to the internet, then click **Try again**.

## Log in with Skool
> Creatorize comes with the Creatorize: AI Video Mastery community. Log in with your Skool account to start.

Fix: click **Log in with Skool** and sign in with the account you use in the community.

## Not a member
> This Skool account isn't in Creatorize: AI Video Mastery, so Creatorize stays locked.

What it means: your membership could not be confirmed for this Skool account.

Fix:
1. Signed in with another account by mistake? Click **Use another Skool account**.
2. Membership ended? Click **Join on Skool**, rejoin, then **Try again**.

## A Premium feature
> ... is a Premium feature. Upgrade to Premium on Skool to unlock it.

What it means: you are on Standard. Premium adds Ultraedit, Routines, custom styles, characters designed with Claude, 4K export, other languages, YouTube Analytics learning and unlimited exports.

Fix: upgrade to Premium on Skool. The app picks up the new plan within about 30 minutes, or right away when you restart it.

## Monthly exports used up
> You've used this month's 12 exports on Standard. They reset on the 1st; Premium has unlimited exports.

Fix: wait until the 1st, or upgrade to Premium on Skool.

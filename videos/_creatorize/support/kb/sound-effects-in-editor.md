Pipeline > **Music & effects** opens three tabs. Or right-click the audio on the timeline > **Add audio layer here (…)…** to open it at that time.

## Layers

Lanes under the voice: **Music**, **Effects**, **Ambience**, plus your own (**New lane**).
- **Add audio layer…** on a lane adds a sound file at the playhead. **From the library…** picks one of your sounds or a built-in one.
- Drag a clip to move it (here or on the timeline). It snaps to words, shots and cues; dropped on a word it stays with that word, also when pauses are trimmed.
- Click a clip to change **Start**, **Trim start**, **Length**, **Fade in**, **Fade out**, **Volume**, **Pan** and **Duck under voice**, tick **Loop to fill**, **Anchor to the word "…"**, move it to another lane, or **Remove** it.
- Each lane has **M** (mute), **S** (solo: only solo lanes play) and a volume slider.
- Ctrl/⌘ + Z undoes a change, Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Z redoes it.

Changes are mixed in within seconds, without Claude: play the preview to hear them.

## Library

Search by name or tag, pick a category, and **All**, **Yours** or **Built-in**. The play button listens. Choose a lane in **Add to** and click **Add to project** (it lands at the playhead).

Add your own sounds with **Add a folder…**, **Add files…** or by dropping files (wav, mp3, ogg, flac, m4a). Subfolders become categories ("Whooshes", "Impacts"), words in file names become tags. **Rescan** finds files you put into the library folder yourself. The same library is in **Settings > Media > Sound effects library**.

# Flat explainer look settings
keywords: flat explainer, flat, flat vector, cartoon, captions, subtitles, caption size, captions too big, captions too small, turn off captions, no captions, film grain, grain, grainy, noise, vignette, dark edges, muddy, camera push-in, zoom, punch-in, cut punch-in, transition length, studio look, brand colour, highlighted word, format
platforms: both

Change these in the editor: **Look** button > change the setting > **Apply to project**. The Flat explainer has one
built-in look, **Studio** (defaults below).

- **Format**: Vertical 9:16 or Wide 16:9. Picked in New project, fixed afterwards.
- **Captions** (on): word-highlight captions in their own band near the bottom. Turn off for no captions.
- **Caption size** (44 to 72, default 56): smaller or bigger caption text.
- **Film grain** (0 to 0.12, default 0.07): the soft film noise. Too grainy? Lower it or set it to 0.
- **Vignette** (0 to 0.3, default 0.16): darker edges. Keep it soft: strong darkening turns warm backgrounds muddy.
  Set to 0 for none.
- **Camera push-in** (0% to 10%, default 5%): how much every shot slowly pushes in. 0% = still camera.
- **Cut punch-in** (0% to 6%, default 3%): a small zoom that eases out right after each cut. 0% = none.
- **Transition length** (0.20s to 0.80s, default 0.42s): the dissolve between scenes.
- **Brand colour** (Style default): your channel colour for the spoken word in the captions, tags, title and chapter
  cards, highlights and the end card.

## Main character

The main character is not a look setting. Pick it in New project; switch it on or off later in the editor's
**Project settings** > **Main character**. See "Characters".

## Make it your default

On the **Styles** page, **Duplicate** the Studio look, change it, **Save look**, then pick it in New project. See
"Styles page".
